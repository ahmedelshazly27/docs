Method note: every official and vendor page I tried to fetch was blocked from this sandbox (DNS failure): gaca.gov.sa, terra-drone.com.sa, flygaca.com, 9tenthsco.com and aleqt.com. **No primary regulatory text was read directly.** Everything below comes from search-result snippets and summaries of the cited URLs. ## 1. GACA drone regulations (GACAR Part 107 / Part 48, 2020–2026)

### Takeaway
Saudi civil drones are governed by GACAR Part 107 (non-recreational small UAS, now reportedly Edition/Version 5, around Jan 2026) and GACAR Part 48 (registration). The rules are risk-based, with "Open" and "Specific" categories. Night flights, flights over people and most event or advertising scenarios fall in the **Specific** category, which needs GACA operational authorization and pilot endorsements. Commercial use also needs drone registration, a GACA remote pilot certificate and site-specific GACA approval through GACA's e-aviation UAS portal.

### Cited Findings
- GACAR Part 107 is titled "Non-recreational civil small unmanned aircraft systems". GACA hosts a v4 PDF and a Part 107 page. Subpart C covers remote pilot certification for the Open and Specific categories — [GACA Part 107 PDF](https://new.gaca.gov.sa/-/media/Files/PDF/LawsAndRegulation/Aviation-Safety-and-Environmental-Sustainability/GACAR-Safety-Regulations/Part-107---Non-recreational-civil-small-unmanned-aircraft-systems.pdf); [GACA 107 v4](https://www.gaca.gov.sa/-/media/Files/PDF/LawsAndRegulation/Aviation-Safety-and-Environmental-Sustainability/GACAR-Safety-Regulations/107-v4.pdf); [GACAR chapter index](https://gaca.gov.sa/web/en-gb/page/gacar-safety-regulations-chapter-index) (not fetched)
- UAS registration falls under GACAR Part 48 and is done electronically through GACA's UAS portal. Both recreational and commercial drones must be registered — [GACA Part 48](https://www.gaca.gov.sa/-/media/Files/PDF/LawsAndRegulation/Aviation-Safety-and-Environmental-Sustainability/GACAR-Safety-Regulations/part-48-gacar.pdf); [9tenths guide (2026)](https://9tenthsco.com/how-to-register-drones-with-gaca-in-saudi-arabia-2025-guide/)
- GACA advisory circulars AC 107-01 ("Advanced Operations with UAS") and AC 107-02 ("Remote Pilot Certification") exist. AC 107-01 lists **night operations** as an example of Specific-category operations, and places Specific-category requirements in Part 107 Subpart D. AC 107-02 says remote pilots need **endorsement training** before certain advanced operations — [AC 107-01](https://gaca.gov.sa/-/media/Files/PDF/LawsAndRegulation/Aviation-Safety-and-Environmental-Sustainability/Safety-and-Aviation-Standards-Advisory-Circulars/GACA-AC-107-01-Advanced-Operations-with-Unmanned-Aircraft-Systems.pdf); [AC 107-02](https://gaca.gov.sa/-/media/Files/PDF/LawsAndRegulation/Aviation-Safety-and-Environmental-Sustainability/Safety-and-Aviation-Standards-Advisory-Circulars/GACA-AC-107-02-Remote-Pilot-Certification.pdf)
- **Version 5 (vendor report, 20 Jan 2026):** Part 107 V5 replaces case-by-case approvals with a risk-based framework. It formalises the Open and Specific categories and introduces **Standard Scenarios (STS-01, STS-02)** as a route to operational authorization. Terra Drone also says V5 sets **Direct and Network Remote ID** requirements, and another site says these applied from January 2026 — [Terra Drone Arabia](https://terra-drone.com.sa/major-update-on-gaca-regulation-part-107-operation-of-uas-v5/); [unmannedairspace.info](https://www.unmannedairspace.info/?p=36573)
- One secondary source says V5 Open-category flying needs an Open Remote Pilot Certificate, registration and a "Permit to Operate". Terra Drone's pages instead say the Open category needs no prior operational authorization, and that drones of 15–25 kg need registration and approval. **The two conflict.** — [artrabia](https://artrabia.com/uas-uav-regulations-in-saudi-arabia-how-easy-is-it-to-fly-today/); [Terra Drone checklist](https://terra-drone.com.sa/drone-permit-in-saudi-arabia-5-step-gaca-checklist/)
- **Altitude:** one guide (Fly GACA) cites §107.59 as setting a maximum of **400 ft (120 m) AGL**. I did not see this in an official GACA document. Another guide cites a 150 m stand-off from residential, commercial or industrial areas for Open operations — [Fly GACA](https://www.flygaca.com/guides/drone-uas-rules-in-ksa/); [madardrone](https://madardrone.com/blog/drone-laws-saudi-arabia/)
- **Over people and BVLOS:** guides say flights over populated sites or beyond visual line of sight (BVLOS) need formal Specific-category authorization, and that night flying is prohibited in the Open category — [Shaaheen Drones guide](https://shaaheen.sa/en/blog/a-comprehensive-guide-to-operating-drones-in-saudi-arabia-gacar-part-107/a-2108200842); [Fly GACA](https://www.flygaca.com/guides/drone-uas-rules-in-ksa/)
- **Dropping objects:** a drone-law aggregator says Saudi rules bar reckless operation that endangers life or property, "including dropping objects from the aircraft". Operations must stay within areas authorised by the GACA air-traffic provider. The same site claims commercial operation needs "GACA security division authorization" (single source) — [drone-laws.com](https://drone-laws.com/drone-laws-in-saudi-arabia/); [UAV Coach](https://uavcoach.com/drone-laws-in-saudi-arabia/)
- **Commercial process (Okaz, 2020, older):** commercial use has two steps: (1) register the drone and get an operating permit, then (2) get GACA approval to use the drone at a specific site. Applications are made only through eaviation.gaca.gov.sa/uas. GACA said at the time that it would issue a comprehensive update to the regulations — [Okaz 2037408](https://www.okaz.com.sa/news/local/2037408)
- **Fees (2020, older; may be outdated):** commercial drone registration SAR 500, operator permit exam SAR 500, permit card SAR 300. These were reported as one-off fees with free renewal — [Al-Muraba](https://www.almuraba.net/?p=254191). In 2020, Arab News reported about SAR 250 for a recreational permit — [Arab News](https://arabnews.com/node/1435026/{{)
- In 2020, GACA began issuing permits and said it had issued thousands. Recreational permits required no exam; commercial permits required a test — [Saudi Gazette 2020](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/596734/SAUDI-ARABIA/GACA-issues-thousands-of-drone-permits); [Arab News 2020](https://www.arabnews.com/saudi-arabia/saudi-civil-aviation-authority-starts-issuing-drone-permits-1482511)
- **Insurance (2020, older):** insurance cover became available for licensed drones, but only for uses such as aerial survey and aerial photography in designated places. Advertising is not listed — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/banking/licensed-drones-now-have-insurance-coverage-in-saudi-arabia-1.1598336379999)
- **Towing, banners, swarms and light shows:** I found no GACA-specific rule in the search results — [search summary over drone-laws.com / UAV Coach](https://drone-laws.com/drone-laws-in-saudi-arabia/)

### Inferences
- A banner-towing or screen-carrying drone over streets or crowds would almost certainly be a **Specific-category** operation. It would fly over people, often at night, carry an external load and fly in urban airspace. That points to a Specific authorization (an SORA-style risk assessment, or an STS if one fits), pilot endorsements, Remote ID compliance and site approval for each event. There is no evidence of an off-the-shelf permit for "advertising drones".
- An external banner or screen counts as an external load and creates a falling-object risk over people, which the reckless-operation and "dropping objects" provisions target. Expect GACA to resist street-level banner flights. Controlled event venues with geofencing and crowd separation are the realistic setting.
- The fees above are 2020 figures and were likely restructured under V5. Treat them as indicative only.

### Gaps
- I could not read the actual Part 107 Edition 5 text: altitude limit, weight classes, over-people rules, external-load and towing rules, multiple drones per pilot, or insurance obligations. gaca.gov.sa was blocked.
- I found no current 2025–2026 GACA fee schedule.
- I could not confirm the portal's current name or URL (2020 reports give eaviation.gaca.gov.sa/uas).

## 2. Security/Interior approvals, import/ownership and penalties

### Takeaway
GACA is the licensing authority. The Ministry of Interior's role surfaced mainly as a security actor: it imposed the 2018 permit requirement after a drone incident near a royal palace. Import reportedly requires GACA registration or an import certificate before Saudi Customs will clear a drone. Penalties under the Civil Aviation Law reach one year in prison and/or a SAR 100,000 fine, and enforcement figures are published quarterly.

### Cited Findings
- Since 2015, operating remotely controlled drones of any size without prior permits has been illegal in Saudi Arabia — [GACA news (via search)](https://gaca.gov.sa/web/en-gb/news/gaca-bans-all-drones-in-kingdom-airspace)
- In April 2018, after a drone was shot down near a royal palace in Riyadh, the Interior Ministry told drone users to obtain permission until regulations were finalised — [Al Jazeera 2018](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2018/4/22/saudi-drone-enthusiasts-to-require-permit-after-palace-incident)
- **Import (2020 reporting):** importers need an operator permit and the drone's serial number, must register through GACA, and receive a GACA import certificate. Saudi Customs then allows registered drones in. Foreign residents reported difficulty registering without an iqama — [Saudi Gazette 2020](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/596734/SAUDI-ARABIA/GACA-issues-thousands-of-drone-permits); [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/legal/saudi-gaca-issues-thousands-of-drone-permits-kqz7l9p3)
- A drone-show vendor says shows need GACA airspace and event approvals, registration of drones over 250 g, and **import permits** for drones brought into the country — [Cyberdrone KSA FAQ](https://www.cyberdrone.com/drone-show-saudi-arabia)
- **Penalties:** in 2020 GACA listed 11 violations, including operating without a licence or permit. The penalty is up to one year in prison, a fine of up to SAR 100,000, or both — [Saudi Gazette 2020](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/596734/SAUDI-ARABIA/GACA-issues-thousands-of-drone-permits); [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/news/local/2037408). A travel guide gives SAR 1,000–50,000 plus confiscation; that source is less authoritative — [drone-traveller](https://drone-traveller.com/drone-laws-saudi-arabia/)
- **Enforcement data:** GACA's Q3 2024 violations report included 4 cases of individuals flying drones without a GACA permit, with fines totalling SAR 25,000. The Q3 2025 report covered 246 violations across all aviation, with fines over SAR 4.8 million, and no drone breakdown was shown in the results — [Okaz 2233440](https://www.okaz.com.sa/local/na/2233440); [Al-Watan](https://www.alwatan.com.sa/ampArticle/1154535) (exact attribution between these two articles was not verified)

### Inferences
- A current Absher or Ministry of Interior drone licence did not surface. Today's process appears to run through GACA, with security coordination behind it. This is unverified, because one aggregator mentions "GACA security division authorization".
- A Kuwaiti company would likely need a Saudi entity (CR) and Saudi-resident certified pilots. Individuals without an iqama were reported to struggle to register, which is old evidence.

### Gaps
- I found no current official rule on Ministry of Interior, Absher or security-clearance steps for commercial drone operators, or on the customs HS-code or import-licence procedure in 2025–2026.
- I found no drone-specific fine schedule under the V5 regime.

## 3. Drone light shows and drone ads in KSA: operators and approvals

### Takeaway
Drone light shows, including **brand logos and QR codes**, are well established in KSA: Ceer EV in 2022, stc in 2026, Riyadh Season, Noor Riyadh and national days. They are run by GACA-licensed local or regional show companies, mostly for GEA, Riyadh Season or government clients. Only a vendor source describes the approvals (GACA airspace and event approval plus import permits). There is no public record of a GEA or municipal ad permit for a show, and **no drone banner-towing advertising operator was found in KSA.**

### Cited Findings
- **Ceer launch (2022):** 1,346 drones at Boulevard Riyadh City formed brand imagery and a scannable QR code, and set a Guinness record. This is a clear commercial brand activation — [Arabad Online](https://arabadonline.com/en/article/3693-Ceer-Saudi-Arabias-first-EV-brand-launches-in-spectacular-style-)
- **stc Group, Founding Day, 22 Feb 2026:** a drone show over Riyadh set two Guinness records, for the largest drone QR code and the largest animated aerial image. The operator was not named in the results — [Leaders MENA](https://www.leaders-mena.com/founding-day-stc-group-breaks-two-guinness-records-with-historic-drone-display/); [Scene Now](https://scenenow.com/News/stc-Group-Sets-Two-Guinness-World-Records-With-Riyadh-Drone-Show)
- Riyadh Season shows displayed the Saudi emblem, leadership images and the **Riyadh Season logo** in 3D. The 2022 Riyadh Season opening set a record for drones launching fireworks — [Arab News](https://arabnews.com/node/2046311/amp); [Saudi Gazette](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/626225/SAUDI-ARABIA/Riyadh-opens-its-3rd-season-by-entering-Guinness-Book-of-Records)
- Noor Riyadh 2022 set a record of 1,990 drones in an aerial display. DRIFT's "Desert Swarm" used 3,000 drones — [Guinness World Records](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/news/commercial/2022/11/massive-light-art-festival-in-saudi-arabia-breaks-six-world-records-726790); [My Modern Met](https://mymodernmet.com/drift-desert-swarm-noor-riyadh)
- A "House of the Dragon" branded drone show was staged on Riyadh Boulevard, an entertainment IP promotion — [Arab News PK](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2144281/corporate-news)
- **Cyberdrone** (Riyadh-based, claims a 7,000-drone fleet) ran the SLS Expo 2025 show in Riyadh (600 drones, with the expo logo), the Jeddah AFC Champions League Elite final, national-celebration shows (for example 1,000 drones in Ha'il) and Medina shows. Its FAQ says roughly 300 drones can render logos and text for brand events — [Cyberdrone KSA](https://www.cyberdrone.com/drone-show-saudi-arabia); [SLS Expo](https://www.cyberdrone.com/projects/sls-expo-drone-show); [national shows](https://www.cyberdrone.com/projects/saudi-national-celebration-drone-shows)
- AO (with Fireworks by Grucci) staged the drone display at the WTTC Global Summit in Riyadh (2022) — [TPMEA](https://www.tpmeamagazine.com/ao-fireworks-by-grucci-put-on-spectacular-world-travel-tourism-summit-display)
- **Al-Eqtisadiah (8 Sep 2025):** the founder of a Saudi company **licensed by GACA to perform drone shows** said it had run **157 drone shows in KSA since Oct 2021**, working with Sela, GEA and the National Events Center. Shows start at about **SAR 275,000**. The founder also referred to new GACA and survey-authority rules that would ease drone use. The company's name was not captured in the snippet — [Al-Eqtisadiah](https://www.aleqt.com/2025/09/08/article_2766305.html) (not fetched)
- GEA organises National Day air, sea and land shows and fireworks across cities. Participants listed included GACA, Saudi Air Navigation Services, military bodies and the Ministry of Interior, but drones were not specifically named — [Sabq](https://sabq.org/article/wiqiva7)
- Earlier example: 300 drones in a Jeddah Ramadan spectacle (2017, older) — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/1106811/300-drones-used-jeddah-spectacle-welcoming-ramadan)
- **Sky Elements:** no evidence it has operated in Saudi Arabia. Its reporting is US-only — [DroneDJ](https://dronedj.com/2024/05/27/drone-fireworks-pyro-sky-elements/)
- **Approvals:** the only explicit description is Cyberdrone's: GACA airspace and event approvals, registration of drones over 250 g, and import permits. No GEA requirement is mentioned — [Cyberdrone](https://www.cyberdrone.com/drone-show-saudi-arabia)

### Inferences
- In practice, brand drone shows happen inside licensed events (Boulevard, Riyadh Season, expos) where the venue or event already holds GEA or event permits. The show company holds the GACA licence and handles airspace. A brand buys the show from a licensed operator and does not operate it.
- Pricing of SAR 275k or more per show makes light shows a premium event product, not a recurring street-media channel.
- I found no public example of drones towing banners or carrying screens over Saudi streets. That suggests the format is either not authorised or not commercially established.

### Gaps
- The identity of the Saudi GACA-licensed show company in the Al-Eqtisadiah article, and the operator of the stc 2026 show, were not established.
- I found no official GEA or municipal statement on permits for drone shows.

## 4. Do aerial ads need a Balady/municipal licence or GAMR approval?

### Takeaway
No final MOMAH rule addressing drone advertising was found. However, a **draft MOMAH advertising-signs regulation** reportedly lists "ads on flying objects and balloons" (الإعلانات على المجسمات الطائرة والبالونات) among the ad types it covers within urban-development boundaries. If adopted, aerial ads would need a municipal (Balady) advertising licence on top of GACA approval. GAMR licensing of the advertising business, covered in the earlier report, would apply as for any ad medium.

### Cited Findings
- A MOMAH draft regulation applies to all advertising signs within urban-development boundaries. The ad types it lists include **ads on flying objects/models and balloons**, alongside flags, electronic screens and temporary event ads. The draft also provides for temporary ad permits, which need Ministry of Commerce approval for sale/discount ads and REGA approval for real-estate ads — [Al-Watan 1154617](https://www.alwatan.com.sa/article/1154617); [Al-Watan 1014229](https://www.alwatan.com.sa/a/1014229) (the draft's date and final adoption status were not confirmed)
- No search result tied GACA drone permits to municipal ad licences — [Al-Watan](https://www.alwatan.com.sa/article/1154617)
- For comparison only (not KSA): Dubai Decree No. 6 of 2020 explicitly names aircraft, airships and balloons as advertising media that need a permit — [PCFC](https://pcfc.ae/en/Rules%20and%20Regulations/Trakhees/Civil%20Engineering%20Department/Planning/Circulars/CEDGR-101%20Decree%20No.%206%20-%20Regulating%20of%20Advertisement%20NOC%20within%20Dubai.pdf)
- Makkah municipality publishes technical requirements for advertising signs, which may be useful for comparison — [Holy Makkah municipality PDF](https://sp.holymakkah.gov.sa/Static/Documents/RegulationsAndRequirements/Technical/الاشتراطات%20الفنية%20للوحات%20الدعائية%20والإعلانية.pdf) (not reviewed)

### Inferences
- A third-party branded drone banner or show over a city would likely need: (a) GACA operator and site approval; (b) a municipal or Balady temporary advertising permit (under the draft, "flying objects" are an ad medium); (c) a GAMR-licensed advertising business; and (d) for events, GEA or venue permits. This chain is inferred. No source documents the whole of it.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether the MOMAH regulation listing "flying objects" was enacted, nor its fee for aerial ads.
- I found no GAMR statement on aerial or drone advertising.

## 5. 2024–2026 regulation changes and enforcement news

### Takeaway
The main change is **GACAR Part 107 Edition/Version 5 (around Jan 2026)**. It introduces risk-based Open and Specific categories, Standard Scenarios and Remote ID. Enforcement continues through GACA's quarterly violation reports. GACA also backs a Saudi Drone Exhibition (since 2024) and a postal drone-delivery trial (Sep 2025).

### Cited Findings
- Part 107 V5 (vendor article of 20 Jan 2026): risk-based framework, STS-01 and STS-02, Remote ID — [Terra Drone Arabia](https://terra-drone.com.sa/major-update-on-gaca-regulation-part-107-operation-of-uas-v5/)
- Q3 2024: 4 drone-without-permit violations, SAR 25,000 in fines. Q3 2025: 246 aviation violations in total, more than SAR 4.8m in fines — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/local/na/2233440); [Al-Watan](https://www.alwatan.com.sa/ampArticle/1154535)
- Saudi Arabia ran its first drone parcel-delivery trial in September 2025 — [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/gulf/2025/09/05/saudi-arabia-tests-drone-delivery-for-postal-parcels-in-step-towards-logistics-innovation)
- GACA sponsors the Saudi Drone Exhibition, held since 2024 — [Bayut blog](https://www.bayut.sa/blog/اكتشف-السعودية/المعرض-السعودي-للدرون/); [Arab News PK](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2613896/saudi-arabia)
- A 2025 vendor guide describes GACA rules for BVLOS cargo in cities — [Saudi Logistics Consulting](https://saudilogisticsconsulting.com/insights/article/gaca-drone-cargo-regulations-the-clear-fast-road-to-bvlos-delivery-in-saudi-cities)

### Inferences
- V5's Standard Scenarios make routine commercial work, such as survey, easier. Advertising over crowds is unlikely to fit an STS, so it would still need a bespoke Specific authorization.

### Gaps
- I could not verify V5's effective date, transition period or full text from GACA.
- I found no 2026 enforcement case involving advertising drones.
