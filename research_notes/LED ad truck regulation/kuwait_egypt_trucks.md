# Regulation of Mobile LED Billboard Trucks in Kuwait and Egypt (as of mid-Nov 2026)

**Scope and method.** These notes look only at what is specific to LED ad trucks: vehicles fitted with large digital screens that drive around or park to show third-party ads. Company setup, tax and labour are covered in `/home/user/docs/research_notes/LED delivery ad platform regulation/`. That earlier work is reused only where it bears on trucks, and I mark it "(prior notes)".

**Blocked hosts.** These could not be fetched because DNS was blocked by the session's network allowlist:
- arabtimesonline.com
- pdf.alanba.com.kw
- scenenow.com

The earlier session also could not reach baladia.gov.kw, aljarida.com or timeskuwait.com. Every finding below therefore comes from search-engine extracts of the cited pages, not from a full read of them. Treat exact figures as needing verification.

**Bottom line up front.**
- **No dedicated truck licence found in either country.** Neither country publishes a licence category, fee schedule or technical standard specifically for "LED billboard trucks" (شاحنات إعلانية / عربات إعلانية متنقلة) that I could find.
- **Kuwait** licenses vehicle ads through Kuwait Municipality, with MOI General Traffic Department approval:
  - The Municipality's December 2025 amended Advertising Regulation brings "mobile" and "electronic" media, *including vehicles and screens*, into scope.
  - The closest existing template is the taxi/public-transport "investment ad" licence. Moving TV screens are allowed under it at KD 30/m², plus a KD 1,000 guarantee and Traffic approval.
  - A purpose-built ad truck is therefore probably licensable, but only case by case.
  - Truck movement bans and the 2025 Traffic Law's penalties for unapproved modifications and devices are the main operational risks.
- **Egypt** licenses any ad on a vehicle under Traffic Law Art. 69 bis:
  - The licence comes from the traffic department and is applied for through an MOI-approved advertising joint-stock company with EGP 30m or more of capital.
  - A dedicated ad-only vehicle fleet already operates in Cairo (SeenX, launched c. 2025).
  - There are no published screen, brightness or video-while-moving rules for vehicles.
  - Heavy-truck bans inside Greater Cairo (reported 6am to midnight) mean a light commercial chassis is far safer than a heavy truck.

## 1. Is a dedicated LED ad truck legal, and under which licence category and fees?

### Takeaway
**Kuwait:** no explicit "mobile advertising truck" category is confirmed in published reporting. Vehicle and electronic-screen ads fall under the Municipality's Advertising Regulation (599/2023 as amended in 2024 and Dec 2025). The only clearly documented route for third-party moving screens on vehicles is the taxi/public-transport licence (TR133). A KD 5/month fee for "mobile vehicles licensed by the Ministry of Commerce" was proposed in 2024, but its adoption is unconfirmed.

**Egypt:** Traffic Law Art. 69 bis makes any ad on a vehicle legal *if licensed* by the competent traffic department. The fee is capped at EGP 10,000, the licence lasts up to 3 years, and the application must go through an MOI-registered advertising company. Law 208/2020 covers fixed roadside ads, and I found nothing applying it to vehicles.

### Cited Findings
**Kuwait**
- **Scope of the amended regulation.** The amended Advertising Regulation defines an "advertising medium" as any fixed or mobile, traditional or electronic means, *including vehicles and electronic screens*. The Municipal Council approved it in late Dec 2025. — [Times Kuwait](https://timeskuwait.com/municipal-council-approves-sweeping-amendments-to-advertising-regulations/); [Arab Times](https://www.arabtimesonline.com/news/new-advertising-rules-introduced-in-kuwait-fines-fees-and-standards-updated/) (prior notes)
- **Penalties and fees in the Dec 2025 amendments, as reported:**
  - KD 3,000–5,000 for any commercial ad "by any means" without a Municipality licence.
  - KD 500 for an unlicensed occasion/event ad.
  - KD 100 annual fee for commercial ads through advertising companies.
  - Source: search extract summarising Al-Anba, Dec 2025 — [Al-Anba PDF 23-12-2025](https://pdf.alanba.com.kw/pdf/2025/12/23-12-2025/06.pdf) (host blocked; extract only)
- **2024 proposal for mobile vehicles.** The Municipality proposed a KD 5/month fee for advertising on "mobile vehicles (عربات متنقلة) licensed by the Ministry of Commerce", and KD 75/m²/year for moving electronic ads. Adoption is not confirmed. — [Al-Jarida](https://www.aljarida.com/article/56921); [Arab Times/PressReader, 25 Mar 2024](https://www.pressreader.com/kuwait/arab-times/20240325/281543705922571) (prior notes)
- **Taxi template (TR133).** Third-party "investment" ads on taxis, including moving TV screens, are licensed at:
  - KD 30/m² for moving TV-screen ads.
  - KD 20/m² per face for other ads.
  - A KD 1,000 guarantee.
  - Traffic Department approval and a copy of the vehicle registration.
  - Sources: [baladia.gov.kw TR133](https://www.baladia.gov.kw/sites/ar/Pages/transactionsRequirements/TR133.aspx); [e.gov.kw public-transport service](https://e.gov.kw/sites/kgoarabic/Pages/Services/KM/ApplyingforIssuingAdvertisementLicensesonPublicTransportVehicles.aspx) (prior notes)
- **Electronic-screen fees (fixed sites).** Reported figures differ: KD 75/m²/side/year minimum (Arab Times) versus KD 100/m²/year (Times Kuwait, May 2026). The May 2026 rules also ban electronic boards in residential areas and on bridges. — [Times Kuwait](https://timeskuwait.com/kuwait-tightens-street-advertising-rules-to-boost-road-safety-and-urban-aesthetics/) (prior notes)
- **Kuwait Portal service.** The portal offers an e-service to issue an ad licence, commercial or identifying, with the fee "determined according to the Advertising Regulation and the measurements entered". — [e.gov.kw licence inquiry/e-service](https://e.gov.kw/sites/kgoArabic/Pages/eServices/KM/AdvertisementLicenseInquiry.aspx) (search extract)
- **Content licensing from 2027.** The new Media Regulation Decree-Law 102/2026 adds Ministry of Information licensing for advertising content, effective around April 2027. — [GLA & Co](https://www.glaco.com/blog/kuwaits-new-media-law-one-framework-for-print-broadcast-digital-media-and-influencers/) (prior notes)

**Egypt**
- **Art. 69 bis (added by Law 161/2021).** Any ad on a vehicle needs a licence from the competent traffic section. The fee is capped at EGP 10,000, with bands set by the executive regulations, and the licence lasts up to 3 years and is renewable. — [Ahram Gate](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/3082998.aspx); [Dostor](https://www.dostor.org/3615067) (prior notes)
- **Art. 69 bis 2 (who may apply).** Only MOI-approved advertising companies may apply. Each must be a joint-stock company with at least EGP 30m of issued capital. — [Youm7](https://www.youm7.com/story/2022/1/14/ما-هى-الشركات-التى-يحق-لها-الإعلان-على-المركبات-قانون/5615118) (prior notes)
- **Fines.** EGP 1,000–5,000 per violation, and the vehicle's licence may be cancelled. — [El Balad](https://www.elbalad.news/5399695) (prior notes)
- **2025 MOI statement.** An ad on a vehicle without a licence is a traffic violation, and so is using a vehicle for a purpose other than the one licensed. Owners can regularise by obtaining "licences to advertise for others" (تراخيص إعلان للغير). — [Egypt Telegraph](https://www.egypttelegraph.com/article/201488/بعد-شكوى-سائقي-التوصيل،-الداخلية-وضع-الإعلانات); [El Watan](https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8184906)
- **Decree 889/2024.** It amended Art. 225 of the Traffic Law executive regulations. Per the search extract, the amended paragraph concerns fees for licences and temporary permits, not screens. — [Egypt Telegraph](https://www.egypttelegraph.com/article/201488/بعد-شكوى-سائقي-التوصيل،-الداخلية-وضع-الإعلانات) (extract)
- **Cairo taxi plan (Jul 2026).** Cairo Governorate is moving toward illuminated ads on white taxis, with ads approved on the doors and contracts still to be signed. This shows that lit vehicle ads are being actively licensed. — [Parlmany, 31 Jul 2026](https://www.parlmany.com/News/7/247427/القاهرة-تتجه-لوضع-إعلانات-مضيئة-بالتاكسى-الأبيض)
- **Law 208/2020 (fixed roadside ads).** NAPRA is building a GIS database of roadside ads and their licence status under Law 208/2020. New rules for licensing road signs were issued in Apr 2026. — [Amwal Al-Ghad EN](https://en.amwalalghad.com/?p=229141); [Masrawy, 27 Apr 2026](https://www.masrawy.com/news/news_egypt/details/2026/4/27/2978681/-تنظيم-الإعلانات-وضع-قواعد-وضوابط-جديدة-لترخيص-اللافتات-على-الطرق-)

### Inferences
- **Kuwait route.** The most defensible approach is to apply for a Municipality ad licence for each vehicle, modelled on TR133 (moving screen, per-m² fee per face, guarantee), with a prior General Traffic Department no-objection.
  - For a truck with two 6×3 m faces (36 m²), the TR133 rate would give roughly KD 1,080/yr at KD 30/m². This is my illustrative calculation, not a published truck fee.
  - At the electronic-screen rate of KD 75–100/m², the same truck would pay KD 2,700–3,600/yr.
  - Expect the Municipality to pick one of these by analogy. Pre-clear it in writing.
- **Kuwait unlicensed risk.** Operating unlicensed exposes the operator to the reported KD 3,000–5,000 commercial-ad fine. For an operator this is a bigger risk than traffic fines.
- **Egypt route.** The operator either becomes, or partners with, an MOI-registered ad joint-stock company with EGP 30m capital. An ad-only truck is also arguably "using the vehicle for a purpose other than licensed" unless its registration reflects that use, so the vehicle's licensed purpose should be set as advertising from the start.

### Gaps
- No published Kuwaiti category named "عربات إعلانية متنقلة / شاحنات إعلانية / شاشات متحركة" with its own fee was found. I also could not confirm whether the 2024 KD 5/month mobile-vehicle proposal survived into the Dec 2025 text, or whether the ministerial resolution has been gazetted.
- The Egyptian fee bands under Art. 69 bis (per vehicle or per m²) and the list of MOI-approved vehicle-ad companies were not found.

## 2. Vehicle registration and type approval, dimensions, and truck time/route bans

### Takeaway
Neither country has a published procedure specifically for converting a truck body to carry LED screens and a generator. Both treat unapproved external modifications as offences.

**Kuwait:** Decree-Law 5/2025 fines unauthorised external modifications (up to KD 200 reported) and impounds non-compliant or noisy vehicles for 60 days. Trucks were banned from main roads at peak hours from 1 Sep 2025 to 14 Jun 2026, and the ban was cut to 12:30–3:30 pm in summer 2026.

**Egypt (Cairo):** Cairo heavy-transport vehicles are reportedly banned inside the city, including downtown, from 6 am to midnight, with separate Ring Road bans.

### Cited Findings
**Kuwait**
- **Decree-Law 5/2025.** It raised traffic fines to a range of KD 15–10,000. Violations involving licence plates, tinting, stickers or **external modifications** are fined up to KD 200. — search extract of [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/kuwait/kuwait-enacts-tougher-traffic-laws-and-harsher-penalties-for-violations-1.500022722); [Kuwait Times](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/26783/kuwait/kuwait-enforces-tougher-traffic-laws-tuesday)
- **Non-compliant and noisy vehicles.** Vehicles that breach safety/durability requirements or emit disturbing noise face 60-day impoundment. Al-Jarida headlined a KD 10,000 fine for workshops fitting noisy exhausts. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/kuwait/kuwait-to-impound-noisy-cars-to-curb-reckless-driving-1.500040167); [Al-Jarida](https://www.aljarida.com/article/89011) (headline only)
- **Administrative impoundment.** Administrative Circular 9/2025 allows two-month impoundment for, among other things, **parking in prohibited places** and deliberately obstructing the road. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/kuwait/kuwait-imposes-tougher-penalties-on-dangerous-driving-vehicles-to-be-impounded-up-to-two-months-1.500318661) (search extract)
- **Screens controlled by the driver.** Electronic boards on rear glass that the driver controls are a "serious" violation, leading to traffic detention and impoundment. — [Al-Jarida, Dec 2023](https://www.aljarida.com/article/47209) (prior notes)
- **Technical inspection.** Renewing a vehicle licence includes a technical inspection, with a KD 5 fee. — [e.gov.kw RenewVehicleLicense](https://e.gov.kw/sites/kgoArabic/Pages/Services/MOI/RenewVehicleLicense.aspx) (search extract)
- **Truck bans, 2025–26 season.** The MOI banned trucks from main roads at peak hours from 1 Sep 2025 to 14 Jun 2026, announced 30 Aug 2025. — [Arab Times](https://www.arabtimesonline.com/news/kuwait-to-ban-trucks-on-main-roads-during-peak-hours-from-sept-1) (host blocked; extract)
- **Truck bans, Ramadan and summer 2026.** Peak-hour bans also applied during Ramadan. For 15 Jun–31 Aug 2026 the ban was cut to 12:30–3:30 pm. — [Arab Times, Ramadan](https://www.arabtimesonline.com/news/trucks-banned-during-peak-hours-in-ramadan-across-kuwait); [Al-Rai](https://www.alraimedia.com/ampArticle/1738312) (extract)
- **Wafra Highway exception.** Ban hours on Wafra Highway (306) were cut to 6–8 am and 1–3 pm. The report is undated. — [Kuwait Times](https://kuwaittimes.com/new-truck-ban-hours-on-wafra-highway-306/major-general-abdullah-al-muhanna-3/)
- **Residents' complaints.** Residents have complained about trucks and buses parked at random in residential areas (Jun 2026). — [Arab Times](https://www.arabtimesonline.com/news/random-parking-of-trucks-buses-irks-area-residents/) (headline)
- **Delivery-vehicle plates.** Delivery vehicles carry yellow (commercial) plates and must display company stickers. — [Court Cast](https://courtscast.com/kuwait-revamps-delivery-vehicle-regulations-what-you-need-to-know/) (prior notes)

**Egypt**
- **Cairo heavy-transport ban.** In Apr 2026 the Cairo Chamber of Commerce asked for the Greater Cairo rules to be changed. It said heavy transport is banned inside cities, including downtown, **from 6 am to midnight**, and proposed allowing movement during working hours for deliveries. — [Maspero, 17 Apr 2026](https://www.maspero.eg/economy/2026/04/17/946313/غرفة-القاهرة-تطالب-بتعديل-سير-سيارات-النقل-الثقيل)
- **Ring Road.** A ban on heavy transport on the Ring Road from 6 am to 12 pm was reapplied, enforced by checkpoints and patrols. These reports are undated. — [Hatla2ee](https://eg.hatla2ee.com/news/60166/حظر-سير-النقل-الثقيل-على-الدائري-من-6-ص-الي-12-م/); [Hatla2ee](https://eg.hatla2ee.com/news/50454/أكمنة-مرورية-ودوريات-على-الدائري-لضبط-سيارات-النقل-المخالفة-للحظر/); [Mobtada](https://www.mobtada.com/print/769311)
- **Non-compliant lighting.** Non-compliant xenon/LED lighting is targeted with fines and licence withdrawal. This concerns headlamps, and it is unknown whether it applies to ad screens. — [Banker.news](https://banker.news/136650) (prior notes)
- **New traffic law.** A new draft traffic law was still being debated in Jul 2026, and its enactment is unconfirmed. — [Parlmany](https://www.parlmany.com/News/7/231301/النواب-يناقشون-قانون-المرور-الجديد) (prior notes)

### Inferences
- **Chassis choice.** In both countries a light/medium commercial chassis (pickup or box-van class) with a screen body probably avoids "heavy truck" time bans. Where those bans apply by weight or plate class, a heavy truck would be barred from Cairo city streets for 18 hours a day and from Kuwait's main roads at peak hours in season.
- **Conversion approval.** Installing a screen box and generator is an "external modification". Get written MOI Traffic approval and pass a technical inspection before the vehicle is used.
  - In Kuwait, unapproved changes risk fines (up to KD 200) and 60-day impoundment.
  - In Egypt, the risk is licence withdrawal.

### Gaps
- No published Kuwaiti or Egyptian rule was found on body-conversion approval, maximum dimensions or weights for screen bodies, or a plate class for ad vehicles.
- The weight threshold used to define "trucks" in Kuwait's peak-hour bans was not found. Kuwait's ban schedule after 31 Aug 2026 is unconfirmed.
- The exact current Cairo heavy-transport decree (number and hours) was not found. The 6 am–midnight figure comes only from the Chamber's complaint.

## 3. Video while driving, brightness, sound, and parking or stopping to advertise

### Takeaway
No explicit rule in either country was found on playing video on a moving vehicle.

**Kuwait:** the signals point to caution.
- The draft December 2025 rules limited delivery motorcycles to a fixed electronic panel with **non-moving content**, subject to Traffic approval.
- Driver-controlled rear-window screens are a serious violation.
- The taxi template does allow "moving TV screens".

**Egypt:** no screen, brightness or video rules for vehicles exist. Media coverage of dazzling LED billboards suggests brightness is a live enforcement topic.

Parking a truck to advertise is risky in both countries without a site permit. In Kuwait, prohibited parking can mean two-month impoundment.

### Cited Findings
- **Kuwait, delivery motorcycles (draft Dec 2025).** A fixed electronic panel with non-moving content is allowed with General Traffic Department approval. Lighting must be self-contained and fixed. — [Arab Times](https://www.arabtimesonline.com/news/new-advertising-rules-introduced-in-kuwait-fines-fees-and-standards-updated/) (prior notes)
- **Kuwait, taxis/public transport.** A licence category exists explicitly for moving TV-screen ads. — [baladia.gov.kw TR133](https://www.baladia.gov.kw/sites/ar/Pages/transactionsRequirements/TR133.aspx) (prior notes)
- **Kuwait, brightness.** An MP asked about ad noise, lighting strength and brightness standards (questioning brightness criteria). — [Al-Jarida](https://www.aljarida.com/article/113889) (headline extract)
- **Kuwait, spacing and location (May 2026).** Electronic boards on highways must be about 400 m apart, and they are banned in residential areas and on bridges. — [Times Kuwait](https://timeskuwait.com/kuwait-tightens-street-advertising-rules-to-boost-road-safety-and-urban-aesthetics/) (prior notes)
- **Kuwait, parking.** Parking in prohibited places can lead to two-month impoundment under Circular 9/2025. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/kuwait/kuwait-imposes-tougher-penalties-on-dangerous-driving-vehicles-to-be-impounded-up-to-two-months-1.500318661)
- **Egypt, brightness.** Cairo roadside ads use bright LED displays that drivers say strain their eyes at night. — [Asharq](https://asharq.com/reports/153110/إعلانات-الطرق-بالقاهرة-زيادة-في-الإيرادات-وإرهاق-للعيون); [inkl/Economist-syndicated](https://www.inkl.com/news/egypt-s-roads-face-a-new-hazard-flashing-billboards-and-adverts)
- **Regional comparator, Oman (not Kuwait/Egypt).** Oman licenses mobile electronic screens on commercial vehicles on condition that they are not operated while the vehicle is moving. — [gov.om](https://gov.om/w/add-a-mobile-advertisement-electronic-screen-on-a-commercial-vehicle) (prior notes)

### Inferences
- **Kuwait:** budget for the Traffic Department possibly requiring static content, or screens switched off, while the truck is moving. Its reported stance on motorcycles and the GCC pattern (Oman) both point that way. Moving video while parked at a licensed site is the safer business model.
- **Speakers:** treat as a separate risk. Kuwait is actively impounding vehicles for noise, and Egypt's Environment Law 4/1994 sets noise limits (see Q6).
- **Parking:** roadside parking to advertise without a site permit would likely be treated as an unlicensed advertising medium (Municipality/NAPRA) and/or illegal parking. In either country, a mall or private-lot location with the landowner's consent plus the vehicle-ad licence is the lowest-risk model.

### Gaps
- No nits/brightness limits for vehicle screens were found in either country.
- No explicit rule was found on video-while-driving, and no specific fine was found for parking a vehicle in order to advertise.
- Whether the final gazetted Kuwaiti text kept the motorcycle "non-moving content" rule, or extended it to other vehicles, is unknown.

## 4. Event and temporary permits (malls, events, public squares)

### Takeaway
**Kuwait:** the Municipality licenses occasion/event ads and temporary exhibitions. The reported penalty is KD 500 for an unlicensed occasion ad, and a KD 500 temporary-exhibition licence fee was kept. Nothing truck-specific was found.

**Egypt:** I found no event permit specific to a parked LED truck. Public-space use falls to the governorate or NAPRA under Law 208, and private venues are a matter of contract.

### Cited Findings
- **Kuwait, unlicensed occasion ads.** The fine for setting up any occasion ad without a licence is reported as KD 500 (Dec 2025 amendments). — [Al-Anba PDF 23-12-2025](https://pdf.alanba.com.kw/pdf/2025/12/23-12-2025/06.pdf) (extract)
- **Kuwait, temporary exhibitions.** The Municipal Council refused to abolish the KD 500 fee for temporary-exhibition licences. The Kuwait Portal lists a service for licensing a temporary exhibition. — [Al-Anba PDF](https://pdf.alanba.com.kw/pdf/2025/11/14-11-2025/04.pdf); [e.gov.kw](https://e.gov.kw/sites/kgoArabic/Pages/eServices/KM/AdvertisementLicenseInquiry.aspx) (extracts)
- **Kuwait, seasonal ads.** A KD 5/m²/month fee for seasonal ads was proposed. — [Al-Jarida](https://www.aljarida.com/article/56921)
- **Kuwait, e-licensing revenue.** The Municipality collected half a million dinars from electronic ad licensing. — [Al-Rai](https://www.alraimedia.com/article/1700113/محليات/أخبار-محلية/سعود-الدبوس-نصف-مليون-دينار-حصيلة-البلدية-من-ترخيص-الإعلانات-إلكترونيا)

### Inferences
- **Kuwait:** a seasonal or occasion ad licence (per m² per month) is the likeliest temporary route for an LED truck parked at an event. The application would be backed by the venue owner's consent.

### Gaps
- No daily or event-specific fee for mobile screens was found in either country.
- Egypt's process for temporary ads at events or in public squares under Law 208 was not found.

## 5. Existing operators and enforcement

### Takeaway
**Egypt:** at least one dedicated ad-only vehicle fleet exists. SeenX, launched c. 2025, describes "vehicles used only for ads" with GPS-tracked LED screens. Its public material, as surfaced in searches, does not describe its licensing.

**Kuwait:** I found **no named Kuwaiti LED-truck operator**. Only event LED-screen rental firms (e.g. Alhafiz) and fixed DOOH operators turned up. Earlier research found Firefly/Viola Outdoor vehicle-top screens elsewhere in the GCC. No enforcement news specific to LED trucks was found in either country.

### Gaps
- No Kuwaiti search (Arabic or English) surfaced an LED-truck advertising operator.
- SeenX's licensing basis, vehicle type and fleet size were not found.

## 6. Generator, fuel and noise rules

### Takeaway
**Egypt:** Environment Law 4/1994 (amended by Law 9/2009) and its executive regulations (Decree 338/1995, Annex 7) set noise loudness limits and regulate exhaust and smoke. I did not retrieve the numeric limits.

**Kuwait:** the Traffic Department impounds vehicles that make disturbing noise for 60 days under the 2025 regime. I found no Kuwait EPA rule specific to mobile generators.

### Cited Findings
- **Egypt, framework.** Law 4/1994 defines pollution to include noise. Its executive regulations (Decree 338/1995) include Annex 7 on permissible sound loudness and exposure. Law 9/2009 added noise and air-emission standards. — [FAOLEX](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/egy4984E.pdf); [Andersen Egypt copy of Law 4/1994](https://eg.andersen.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Law-No.-4-of-1994.pdf); [ECOLEX, Law 9/2009](https://www.ecolex.org/details/legislation/environmental-law-no9-of-2009-amending-some-provisions-of-law-no4-of-1994-on-environment-lex-faoc152133/)
- **Kuwait, noisy vehicles.** Noisy and non-compliant vehicles face 60-day impoundment. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/kuwait/kuwait-to-impound-noisy-cars-to-curb-reckless-driving-1.500040167)

### Inferences
- **Generator choice.** A quiet, enclosed generator or a battery/inverter system reduces noise risk in both countries. Avoid external speakers, or keep them off, except at permitted events.

### Gaps
- The Annex 7 dB values were not retrieved.
- No Kuwait EPA (Law 42/2014) provisions on mobile generators or noise were found in this session.
