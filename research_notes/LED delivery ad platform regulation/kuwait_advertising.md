# Kuwait: Regulation of Digital/Mobile Outdoor Advertising (LED film on delivery boxes and shop windows), as of Nov 2026

**Method note:** Direct page fetches were blocked in this research session. The blocked hosts were baladia.gov.kw, aljarida.com, arabtimesonline.com, timeskuwait.com and lexis.ae. Every finding below therefore comes from search-engine extracts of the cited pages, not from a full read of them. The figures should be re-checked against the full articles and against the official text in Kuwait Al-Youm (the official gazette) before anyone relies on them. Dates are approximate where the extract did not give one.

## Q1. Which authority licenses outdoor/advertising signs, and what is the current regulation?

### Takeaway
Kuwait Municipality (Baladia) licenses all outdoor advertising, through its Advertisement Licensing Section. Its rules are in the "Advertising Regulation" (لائحة الإعلانات):
- **Base text:** Ministerial Resolution No. 599/2023.
- **First amendment:** Resolution No. 223/2024.
- **Second amendment:** a further set of amendments approved by the Municipal Council in December 2025. I could not confirm that the ministerial resolution giving effect to them has been published in the gazette.

The new Media Regulation Decree-Law No. 102/2026 (Ministry of Information) adds a separate layer for licensing advertising and promotional content. It takes effect around April 2027.

### Inferences
- The operating stack in force is probably:
  - Resolution 599/2023, as amended by 223/2024.
  - The December 2025 amendments, if they have been issued by ministerial resolution. The 2026 enforcement reporting suggests they are being applied.
  - From around April 2027, Decree-Law 102/2026, plus its executive regulations, will apply to promotional content.
- The Media Law is aimed mainly at media, digital and social channels. Whether a Ministry of Information advertising licence will also be needed for DOOH content is still open; see Gaps.

### Gaps
- I found no gazette citation (resolution number or date) for the ministerial resolution that gives effect to the December 2025 Municipal Council amendments.
- The full text of the in-force Advertising Regulation could not be retrieved (baladia.gov.kw was blocked).
- The executive regulations of Decree-Law 102/2026 are not yet issued (due around April 2027). Their treatment of out-of-home and digital screens is unknown.

## Q2. Are vehicle ads permitted? Are illuminated, digital or moving screens on vehicles allowed?

### Takeaway
Ads on vehicles are licensable by the Municipality. However:
- **Delivery motorcycles:** the December 2025 amendments reportedly limit them to ads identifying the operator's own activity ("الإعلان التعريفي للنشاط فقط"), for KD 40/year. A screen is allowed only if it is a fixed electronic panel with non-moving content and static self-contained lighting, and General Traffic Department (Ministry of Interior) approval is required.
- **Taxis:** "investment" (third-party) ads, including moving TV-screen ads, have an existing Municipality licence route (KD 30/m²), subject to Traffic Department approval.
- **Car screens with no approval:** the Traffic Department treats electronic screens/boards mounted on car windows as a serious violation.

A moving-video LED box on a delivery motorcycle that carries third-party ads therefore looks non-compliant under the reported rules.

### Cited Findings
- **Delivery motorcycles, December 2025 amendments as reported:**
  - KD 40/year for ads on delivery-service motorcycles, "for the identifying advertisement of the activity only".
  - KD 100/year for commercial advertisements placed through advertising/publicity companies, "the fee being due for the full licence of any advertisement".
  - The ad on a delivery motorcycle "may be a fixed electronic panel with non-moving content, provided approval of the Ministry of Interior – General Traffic Department is submitted", and "lighting must be self-contained and fixed".
  - Advertising companies must be registered, qualified or classified with the Municipality.
  - — [Al-Anba (c. Oct 2025)](https://www.alanba.com.kw/1329133); [Al-Rai: "new Advertising Regulation… fines up to 5000 KD"](https://www.alraimedia.com/article/1751030/%D9%85%D8%AD%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA/%D8%A3%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%85%D8%AD%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9/%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%B9%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D8%BA%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AD%D8%AA%D9%89-5000-%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%84%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AE%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%81%D9%8A%D9%86)
  - The 100 KD clause could be read either as a separate category or as applying to vehicles generally. The extract is ambiguous.
- **Delivery motorcycles, older rule (Resolution 223/2024):**
  - Up to 5 m² of ad area: KD 50/year.
  - Over 5 m² and up to 10 m²: KD 100/year.
  - — [lawskw.com, 223/2024](https://lawskw.com/section/%D8%A8%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%AA-%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-599-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2023-%D8%A8%D8%B4%D8%A7%D9%86-%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%84%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%A6%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-223-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2024-%D8%A8%D8%B4%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%84-%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA)
- **Taxis and delivery vehicles:** Ads must be confined to the vehicle's sides without protruding. Design plans must be approved by a Municipality-accredited engineering office. — [Arab Times](https://www.arabtimesonline.com/news/new-advertising-rules-introduced-in-kuwait-fines-fees-and-standards-updated/); [Times Kuwait](https://timeskuwait.com/municipal-council-approves-sweeping-amendments-to-advertising-regulations/)
- **Taxi third-party ads ("investment ads"), official Municipality service page:**

    - KD 30/m² per ad with a moving (TV) screen.
    - KD 20/m² per face for investment/variable ads.
    - KD 1,000 financial guarantee covering all ad licences.
  - Requirements:
    - Approval of the General Traffic Department (MOI).
    - Monitoring by the Municipality's ad inspectors.
    - A copy of the valid vehicle registration book.
  - — [Kuwait Municipality TR133 "Taxi advertisement licence"](https://www.baladia.gov.kw/sites/ar/Pages/transactionsRequirements/TR133.aspx)
- **Public-transport vehicles (official e-gov service):** The same fee structure applies (KD 30/m² for moving TV screens, KD 20/m² per face, KD 1,000 guarantee). - **Renewal on private transport:** Renewal fees for commercial ads on all private means of transport are reported as KD 5/m² per face. — secondary site summary in search ([kuwaitarchive.net](https://kuwaitarchive.net/advertisement-license/)); not verified

- **Traffic Department position (9 Dec 2023):**
  - Electronic boards/screens fitted to vehicles' rear glass that are controlled by the driver are a serious ("جسيمة") traffic violation.
  - The driver is referred to traffic detention and the vehicle is impounded.
  - If the content is indecent, the driver is referred to a police station and a case is opened.

- **Traffic law penalties (Decree-Law 5/2025, in force 22 Apr 2025):**
  - Unauthorised changes to plates, tint, stickers or external features: fines up to KD 200. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/kuwait/kuwait-enacts-tougher-traffic-laws-and-harsher-penalties-for-violations-1.500022722)
  - A secondary fines list gives KD 50 for stickers or writing on a vehicle. — [fines list PDF](https://kuwait.it.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Kuwait-Traffic-Fines-List-pdf.pdf)
  - The Traffic Department has warned against unauthorised stickers, writings or images on vehicle bodies or windows. — [Kuwait Times](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/45769/kuwait/other-news/warning-against-unauthorized-vehicle-stickers/)
- **Comparator, not Kuwait:** Oman's government portal has a service for licensing a mobile electronic screen ad on a commercial vehicle, with conditions such as not operating it while the vehicle is moving. This shows the regional regulatory pattern. — [gov.om](https://gov.om/w/add-a-mobile-advertisement-electronic-screen-on-a-commercial-vehicle)

### Inferences
- **Delivery motorcycles.** Under the reported December 2025 text, three things are needed:
  - Municipality licence.
  - General Traffic Department approval.
  - Static, non-moving content.

  The "identifying ad of the activity only" wording suggests a delivery motorcycle may carry the delivery operator's own branding, not rotating third-party ads. The separate KD 100/year "commercial ads via advertising companies" category may offer a route for third-party ads, but the extracts do not make clear that it covers motorcycles. Clarify this with the Municipality's Advertisement Licensing Section.
- **Delivery cars.** These have no explicit reported screen rule. The taxi/public-transport model shows that third-party moving screens on vehicles can be licensed (KD 30/m² plus KD 1,000 guarantee plus Traffic approval). There is no confirmed pathway for privately owned delivery cars.
- **Traffic exposure.** Screens put on vehicles without Traffic Department approval risk impoundment and a serious-violation referral. That risk falls on the riders, which is a material operational and reputational risk for a model that pays riders.
- **Brightness and video.** For vehicles, the only content-motion rule found is "non-moving content" for motorcycles. No numeric brightness (nits) or size limit specific to vehicles was found.

### Gaps
- No numeric brightness, size or dimension limits for vehicle screens were found in Kuwaiti sources.
- It is unconfirmed whether the final December 2025 text kept the "identifying ad only" restriction and the "non-moving content" rule.
- The General Traffic Department's criteria for approving vehicle-mounted screens are unknown; I found no published MOI standard.

## Q3. Digital screens on shop facades and windows

### Takeaway
Every facade sign or screen needs a Municipality licence, in practice per sign/medium. The reported conditions are:
- Design approved by an accredited engineering office.
- Fees charged per m² per display side for electronic screens: KD 75 or KD 100/m²/year, depending on the source.
- The licence's validity is tied to the shop's commercial licence.
- Illuminated shopfront boxes may not show moving or animated content.

Whether a shop may host third-party ads on its facade, and whether screens placed inside behind the glass are regulated, was not confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- **Licence route:** The official e-gov service "Issuing Licenses for Advertisements on Store Facades and Light Boxes" requires an application to the Municipality's Advertisement License Section with a photo of the planned ad. — [e.gov.kw](https://e.gov.kw/sites/kgoenglish/Pages/Services/KM/IssuingLicensesForAdvertisementsOnStoreFacadesAndLightBoxes.aspx)
- **Prior approval and removal:** Prior approval is required before any billboard or shop sign, including illuminated signs and digital screens, is installed on a building facade. Unlicensed ads can be removed at the offender's expense. — [Times Kuwait](https://timeskuwait.com/municipality-updates-outdoor-advertisement-regulations/); [Times Kuwait](https://timeskuwait.com/kuwait-municipality-sets-clear-rules-for-advertising-licenses/)
- **Placement:** Shopfront ads go at the top of the facade, at a maximum height of 3 m, and must not obstruct residential views. — [Arab Times](https://www.arabtimesonline.com/news/new-advertising-rules-introduced-in-kuwait-fines-fees-and-standards-updated/)
- **Illuminated boxes:** Size limits apply, lighting must be fixed and self-contained, and moving or animated content is banned. — [Arab Times](https://www.arabtimesonline.com/news/new-advertising-rules-introduced-in-kuwait-fines-fees-and-standards-updated/)
- **Electronic screen classes and fee:** Screens are classed as "moving" (video/text) or "fixed" (non-moving for the whole licence period). The minimum annual fee is KD 75/m² per display side. — [Arab Times](https://www.arabtimesonline.com/news/new-advertising-rules-introduced-in-kuwait-fines-fees-and-standards-updated/)
  - Contradicted by KD 100/m²/year for electronic advertising media. — [Times Kuwait](https://timeskuwait.com/kuwait-tightens-street-advertising-rules-to-boost-road-safety-and-urban-aesthetics/)
- **Licence term and changes:** Facade ad licences share the validity of the related commercial licence and are issued and renewed electronically. No modification is allowed without prior Municipality approval. — [Times Kuwait](https://timeskuwait.com/municipality-finalizes-amendments-to-advertising-regulations-with-stricter-fines-and-new-licensing-rules/)
- **Trade names:** Trade names on billboards must match in colour and size, except for licensed commercial agencies. — [Times Kuwait](https://timeskuwait.com/municipality-updates-outdoor-advertisement-regulations/)
- **Brightness:** Ministry of Interior technical limits on illuminated billboard brightness are enforced, and rooftop and facade ads must meet additional safety and design conditions (Jan 2026). — [Lexis ME](https://www.lexismiddleeast.com/news/2026-01-26_17/en)
  - A Municipal Council member raised questions about brightness standards and nuisance from the proliferation of electronic ads. — [Al-Jarida](https://www.aljarida.com/article/113889)
- **Residential areas:** Electronic billboards are banned in residential areas and on bridges. — [Times Kuwait](https://timeskuwait.com/kuwait-tightens-street-advertising-rules-to-boost-road-safety-and-urban-aesthetics/)
- **Seasonal ads:** KD 5/m² per month. — [Al-Jarida](https://www.aljarida.com/article/56921)

### Inferences
- **Shop-window screens.** Screens facing the street and visible from outside are likely to be treated as facade advertising media and to need individual Municipality licences. A "moving" screen would then pay the electronic-screen fee per m² per side.
- **Third-party ads.** The facade licence is tied to the shop's commercial licence, and the ad must be on the shop's own facade. This structure suggests facade signage is meant for the shop's own activity. Third-party "investment" advertising on private buildings may need a different licence category, possibly requiring the agency to be a registered advertising company. This is unconfirmed.
- **Screens behind the glass.** I found no rule that exempts internal screens facing outward. Do not assume an exemption.

### Gaps
- No source confirmed whether shop owners may lease facade or window space for third-party ads.
- No source addressed the regulatory status of screens mounted inside the shop window and facing out.
- The exact current electronic-screen fee (KD 75 vs KD 100/m²) is unresolved.

## Q4. Content rules (Information, Commerce, Health)

### Takeaway
The content of an ad is regulated separately from the sign licence:
- **Ministry of Information:** historically held a list of banned and restricted commercial ads. It now administers Decree-Law 102/2026, which requires ads to be licensed and clearly disclosed, from around April 2027.
- **Ministry of Commerce and Industry (MOCI):** licenses sales promotions and offers, and real-estate ads.
- **Ministry of Health (MOH):** requires prior approval for medical, pharmacy, cosmetic and health-product ads (Law 70/2020 Art. 15; Resolution 87/2023; Ministerial Decision 9/2025).
- **Arabic:** ads are reportedly required to be in Arabic (secondary source).

### Cited Findings
- **Decree-Law 102/2026, scope:**
  - Licensing and disclosure requirements for advertising and promotional activity.
  - Promotional content must be explicitly and clearly labelled.
  - Content standards cover privacy, children, reputation, religion, national unity and public order.
  - False or misleading information is prohibited, and AI-generated content is addressed.
  - Statements may not be attributed to the Amir or Crown Prince without Diwan authorisation.
  - — [Al Tamimi](https://www.tamimi.com/law-update/the-technology-issue-2026/articles/kuwaits-new-media-law-a-comprehensive-overhaul-of-the-media-regulatory-framework/); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/social-media/1853030/kuwaits-new-media-regulation-law-what-businesses-need-to-know); [Lexis ME](https://www.lexismiddleeast.com/news/2026-10-05_21/en)
- **Decree-Law 102/2026, licensing mechanics:**
  - Licence applications must be decided within 60 days and are deemed approved if no decision is issued.
  - No prior censorship, per the explanatory memorandum.
  - — [ASAR](https://www.asarlegal.com/kuwaits-new-media-regulation-law-what-businesses-need-to-know/); [Al Tamimi](https://www.tamimi.com/law-update/the-technology-issue-2026/articles/kuwaits-new-media-law-a-comprehensive-overhaul-of-the-media-regulatory-framework/)
- **Arabic and prohibited products:** Advertisements must be in Arabic, must not promote prohibited products, and must avoid offensive content. Medicines, stimulants, herbs, cosmetics and health materials under Law 38/2002 cannot be advertised without a Health Ministry licence. — [Kuwait Times via PressReader, 8 Jan 2017](https://www.pressreader.com/kuwait/kuwait-times/20170108/281732679167263) (older rule; may be superseded)
- **Sales promotions:** Promotions must be notified for approval, and the licence number must be displayed on every ad for the promotion. — [Practical Law](https://uk.practicallaw.thomsonreuters.com/w-012-8167)
  - The MOCI e-service "license to publish an advertisement (collecting third-party money)" sets these conditions:
    - The store must already be operating the licensed activity.

    - The ad period is a maximum of 1 month.
    - Applications go to the Consumer Protection Department, Advertisements and Free Offers Dept.

- **Real estate:** MOCI has tightened its policies on real-estate ads. — [Kuwait Times](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/22631/kuwait/other-news/commerce-ministry-tightens-policies-for-real-estate-ads/)

### Inferences
- **Category exclusions.** Alcohol, pork and gambling are prohibited goods or activities in Kuwait, so ads for them would fall under the "prohibited goods" fine band. I found no ad-specific citation listing them; this is general knowledge and should be verified.
- **Pre-clearance by advertiser category:**
  - Health, clinic, cosmetic and supplement advertisers: get MOH pre-approval for each creative.
  - Retail promotions and discount offers: get a MOCI licence number displayed on the ad.
  - Real estate: get MOCI approval.
- **Media Law 102/2026.** It could bring DOOH content under the Ministry of Information's licensing from 2027, depending on the executive regulations.

### Gaps
- No specific Kuwaiti rule on food-ad content was found.
- I found no current Ministry of Information rule requiring pre-approval of every outdoor ad creative.
- The Arabic-language requirement is cited only from a secondary legal overview; I found no primary text.

## Q5. Penalties and enforcement

### Takeaway
The amended Advertising Regulation (December 2025) sets fines of KD 100–5,000:
- KD 3,000–5,000 for commercial ads without a licence.
- Removal at the violator's expense, confiscation, and licence withdrawal after two warnings.

Under Decree-Law 102/2026 (from about April 2027), unlicensed advertising is reported to carry fines of KD 1,000–50,000. Municipal inspection campaigns ran throughout 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **Municipal fines:**
  - Graduated fines of KD 100–5,000.
  - KD 500–3,000 for informational or business ads, or ads promoting prohibited goods.

## Q6. Precedents: delivery-box or taxi-top digital ads in Kuwait

### Takeaway
The Municipality has a formal licence product for third-party, TV-screen ads on taxis and public-transport vehicles, so moving-screen ads on licensed taxis are an established category. However, I found no named Kuwaiti operator running taxi-top or delivery-box digital screens. Regional precedents exist in Abu Dhabi and Dubai.

### Cited Findings
- **Existing Kuwaiti licence route:** Municipality transaction TR133 licenses "investment" ads on taxis, including moving TV screens (KD 30/m²), with Traffic Department approval and a KD 1,000 guarantee. — [baladia.gov.kw TR133](https://www.baladia.gov.kw/sites/ar/Pages/transactionsRequirements/TR133.aspx)
  - A parallel route exists for public-transport vehicles. — [e.gov.kw](https://e.gov.kw/sites/kgoarabic/Pages/Services/KM/ApplyingforIssuingAdvertisementLicensesonPublicTransportVehicles.aspx)
- **Kuwaiti DOOH players found, none on vehicles:**
  - M2R Group (Kuwait) lists DOOH services. — [DesignRush](https://www.designrush.com/agency/profile/m2r-group)
  - Al Salam Khaleej runs a roadside LED network. — [media.co.uk](https://media.co.uk/blogs/blog/digital-competition-al-salam-khaleej-led-positioning)
- **Abu Dhabi:** The Integrated Transport Centre launched smart taxi-top billboards, starting with 50 Tawasul taxis. — [Gulf Business](https://gulfbusiness.com/en/2023/transport/integrated-transport-centre-introduces-initiative)
  - Firefly supplies vehicle-top LED screens via Viola Outdoor. — [Firefly](https://www.fireflyon.com/blog/firefly-supplies-its-market-leading-digital-media-screens-to-viola-outdoor)
- **Talabat in Kuwait:** Talabat runs rider rest stops in Kuwait. This shows the scale of the rider ecosystem but is unrelated to advertising. — [Al-Jarida](https://www.aljarida.com/article/104159)

### Inferences
- The taxi TV-screen licence (TR133) is the closest existing Kuwaiti template for licensing vehicle-mounted video ads. A delivery-box model would probably need either an analogous category or reliance on the KD 100/year "commercial ads via advertising companies" clause. Either way it would need Traffic Department approval.

### Gaps
- I found no Kuwaiti company publicly operating delivery-box or car-top digital screens, and no news coverage of one.
- I found no information on whether any Kuwaiti taxi operator actually uses TR133 for screens today.
