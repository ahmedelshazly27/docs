## 1. MOMRAH advertising requirements (اشتراطات اللوحات الدعائية والإعلانية): vehicles, digital screens, fees, Balady licence, penalties

### Takeaway
Vehicle advertising is a licensable category under MOMRAH's billboard requirements (1443H/2021 version, on momah.gov.sa since 2022-03). The rules cover buses, taxis, light transport vehicles and goods trucks. A taxi roof-mounted sign ("الإعلان المجسم") is expressly contemplated. The licence needs **prior approval from the General Directorate of Traffic** and is processed via Balady. For private/company vehicles, though, the text is restrictive: the ad must be a **sticker only, with no protrusion, on the two sides only**. That rule argues against LED screens on ordinary vehicles unless a specific approval is obtained. Unlicensed ads on vehicles are a per-vehicle municipal violation.

### Cited Findings
- **Scope covers vehicles and screens.** The requirements define ad boards to include electronic screens, "standalone or attached to a facility **or a vehicle**". Content can be written, engraved, stuck on, or shown "on an electronic screen or other technologies". — [MOMRAH requirements 1443H/2021 (Istitlaa PDF)](https://istitlaa.ncc.gov.sa/ar/municipality/momra/paintings/Documents/%D9%85%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B9%20%D8%A7%D8%B4%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B7%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%84%D9%88%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%8A%D8%A9%20%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%B9%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9.pdf); final version hosted at [momah.gov.sa (2022-03)](https://momah.gov.sa/sites/default/files/2022-03/%D8%A7%D8%B4%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B7%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%84%D9%88%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%A9.pdf)
- **Private/company vehicles:** "إعلان ملصق فقط دون بروز · على جانبي السيارة فقط" (a sticker ad only, no protrusion, on the two sides of the vehicle only). The ad must not cover traffic data or block truck lights. — same documents as above
- **Categories permitted:** ads on buses, taxis (سيارات الأجرة), light transport vehicles and goods trucks.
- **Taxi roof signs:** "الإعلان المجسم: هو عبارة عن لوحة يتم تركيبها فوق سقف سيارة الأجرة ويتم تثبيتها بشكل مخفي". In English: a 3-D sign mounted on the taxi roof with concealed fixing. It must not block drivers' vision and must follow the approved model dimensions in the requirements.
- **Traffic approval is mandatory.** The draft says: "يجب الحصول على موافقة الإدارة العامة للمرور قبل إصدار الترخيص على المركبات بجميع أنواعها". The final version says: "...في حال كان الترخيص للمركبات أو الشاحنات". — [Istitlaa project page](https://istitlaa.ncc.gov.sa/ar/Municipality/momra/paintings/Pages/default.aspx); [Istitlaa billboard page](https://istitlaa.ncc.gov.sa/ar/Municipality/momra/billboard/Pages/default.aspx)
- **General conditions.** Boards and their materials must be fire-resistant per SBC 801. Issue, renewal, amendment, suspension and cancellation follow the Municipal Licensing Procedures Law (نظام إجراءات التراخيص البلدية) and its executive regulation. — [Al-Watan: 4 conditions for billboard licences](https://www.alwatan.com.sa/article/1154617)
- **Fixed roadside digital screens (for comparison, not vehicles).**
  - Electronic screens on roads must be at least 500 m apart, and boards at least 5 m from traffic signals. — [MOMRAH requirements via Istitlaa](https://istitlaa.ncc.gov.sa/ar/municipality/momra/billboard)
  - A MOMRAH Furas tender booklet for standing electronic screens caps brightness at **1,000 candela** with a screen angle of 45–90°.
  - The same booklet says the minimum display time should ensure drivers see no more than one or two messages, with near-instant (about 1 s) transitions. That text was OCR-garbled, so verify it. — [Furas booklet: electronic screens](https://furas.momah.gov.sa/sites/default/files/%D9%83%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%A9%20%20%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AB%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B1%20%D9%84%D9%88%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D8%AF%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%8A%D8%A9%20_compressed.pdf)
- **Balady e-service: "إصدار رخصة تشغيلية للوحة دعائية أو إعلانية".**
  - Applications are online via Balady only, and processing takes 1–10 days.
  - Requirements: a valid commercial registration and an investment contract (عقد استثمار). A third-party service site also lists a copy of the commercial licence.
  - The board must be removed when the licence expires. — [MOMAH e-service page](https://momah.gov.sa/en/e-services/issuing-operational-license); [lao.com.sa summary](https://lao.com.sa/%D8%A5%D8%B5%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%B1%D8%AE%D8%B5%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B4%D8%BA%D9%8A%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D9%88%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%AF%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A3%D9%88-%D8%A5%D8%B9%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9/p1455321409)
- **Fees.** A separate instrument exists, "معايير تحديد مقدار رسوم لوحات الدعاية والإعلان" (criteria for setting billboard fees). Its per-vehicle or per-sqm vehicle rate was not retrievable. — [qanoonsa](https://qanoonsa.com/p/503902/)
- **Penalties (municipal violations schedule).**
  - The violation is "وضع لوحة أو ملصق إعلاني على المركبات بدون ترخيص" (placing an ad board or sticker on vehicles without a licence).
  - It is charged **per vehicle**, with the licence holder responsible, classed as non-serious, with a 2-day correction period, and doubled on repeat.
  - Extracted amounts range from **SAR 500 to SAR 2,500**. Which tier applies to a first offence was unclear from the OCR.
  - An unlicensed board on land costs SAR 1,000–1,600 per m². — [MOMAH penalties schedule, modified 2026-03 upload](https://momah.gov.sa/sites/default/files/2026-03/jdwl%20aljzaat%20%60n%20almkhalfat%20albldyt%20-%20m%60dl4.pdf); earlier [Balady 1444H/2023 schedule](https://balady.gov.sa/sites/default/files/2023-06/%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%84%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A1%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D8%B9%D9%86%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AE%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9.pdf); [Istitlaa draft section 6, billboard violations](https://istitlaa.ncc.gov.sa/ar/Municipality/momra/Schedule/Pages/6.-%D9%85%D8%AE%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%84%D9%88%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%B9%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9.aspx)
- **Policy backdrop.** In May 2020 the Council of Ministers approved a strategy to develop outdoor advertising in Saudi cities, under Vision 2030. — cited in coverage of [Remat Al-Riyadh contracts (Al-Eqtisadiah, 30 May 2024)](https://www.aleqt.com/2024/05/30/article_2730781.html)

### Inferences
- **Private-fleet LED trucks or car screens.** The "sticker only, no protrusion, sides only" rule means a bolted-on LED screen on a private or company vehicle does not fit the standard vehicle-ad template. It would likely need express municipal and Traffic approval, or may simply not be licensable as a routine Balady permit.
- **Taxi roof signs** have a defined place in the rules: the الإعلان المجسم. A digital roof box is the most plausible licensable format, but the text extracted describes a "board" (لوحة) and does not expressly mention a digital one.
- **Investment-contract model.** Because Balady lists an investment contract as a prerequisite, outdoor-ad licences (including on vehicles in big cities) appear tied to municipal investment and concession contracts, not open per-vehicle permits. See Section 2.

### Gaps
- No 1445–1447H revised version of the requirements was found. The latest identified is the 1443H/2021 version (published 2022).
- Not found in readable form:
  - exact article numbers
  - vehicle-specific fee rates (per vehicle or per m²)
  - licence duration for vehicle ads
  - brightness and dwell-time rules specific to vehicle-mounted screens
  - the exact first-offence penalty amount
- Direct verification needs access to momah.gov.sa, balady.gov.sa and istitlaa.ncc.gov.sa.

## 2. Mobile advertising trucks and taxi/bus ads: city-level (Riyadh, Jeddah), zones and hours

### Takeaway
In Riyadh, the Amanah treats ads on taxis and buses as a **municipal investment concession**. It runs through Remat Al-Riyadh, the Amanah's development arm, via the Furas platform. A 2024 concession for this went to Fadn Advertising Agency, a subsidiary of Al Arabia, the listed company. No city rules were found specifically for LED screen trucks (permitted zones or hours) in Riyadh or Jeddah.

### Cited Findings
- **Remat Al-Riyadh's tender package.** It covered building facades, plus ads on public taxis and buses (the second phase), via "innovative business models" with the private sector. Offers were made through the Furas municipal investment portal. — [Sabq](https://sabq.org/article/d7AoX5l); [Al-Eqtisadiah, 30 May 2024](https://www.aleqt.com/2024/05/30/article_2730781.html)
- **Fadn Advertising Agency** (100% owned by Al Arabia / الشركة العربية للتعهدات الفنية) won both Riyadh contracts to "build, operate and maintain ad boards on public taxis and buses in Riyadh".
  - The project consists of **advertising stickers on 500 vehicles** (taxis or buses).
  - Term: 2 years for each contract.
  - Values: contract 1 SAR 6.84m excl. VAT; contract 2 about SAR 5.795m. The second was signed 30 May 2024.
  - Sources: [Saudi Exchange (Tadawul) announcement](https://www.saudiexchange.sa/wps/portal/saudiexchange/newsandreports/issuer-news/issuer-announcements/issuer-announcements-details/?locale=enar&anCat=91&anId=77778&cs=4071); [Argaam: second contract SAR 5.8m](https://www.argaam.com/ar/article/articledetail/id/1697184); [Argaam: 3 contracts SAR 442.6m](https://www.argaam.com/ar/article/articledetail/id/1731857)
- **Total value.** Five Fadn contracts with Remat (building facades plus taxis and buses) totalled more than SAR 442m in revenue over their terms. — [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/ar/article/articledetail/id/1731857); [Makkah newspaper](https://makkahnewspaper.com/article/1614491)
- **Older report (about 2022–23).** Riyadh would allow advertising on building fronts, taxis and buses "for the first time". — [Arabian Business](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/industries/media/riyadh-announces-it-will-allow-advertising-on-building-fronts-taxis-and-buses) (older item)
- **Enforcement against unlicensed ads.** Riyadh Amanah traces people who post unlicensed ad stickers through the phone numbers printed on them. — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/news/local/2091496)
- **Truck market.** Searches for Saudi LED screen-truck operators turned up only event LED-screen rental listings, such as [Haraj](https://haraj.com.sa/11183357000/LED_Screen_Rental/). No identified operator was publicly running licensed roaming LED ad trucks.

### Inferences
- **Concession lock-in.** In Riyadh, and possibly other big Amanahs, taxi and bus exteriors appear to be a concessioned ad inventory. A new entrant would likely need to bid through Furas or Remat, or partner with the concession holder (Fadn/Al Arabia), rather than simply license screens per vehicle.
- **Stickers, not screens.** The 2024 Riyadh concession was for stickers. Digital taxi-top screens would probably need a new tender or a contract variation. The 2-year terms signed in mid-2024 would expire around mid-2026, so a re-tender may be current or imminent. This is unverified.
- **Truck permitting.** Roaming LED trucks are probably handled case by case (Amanah licence plus Traffic approval), likely for events or campaigns. No published zone or hour rules were found.

### Gaps
- No Riyadh or Jeddah Amanah regulation found that names "شاحنات إعلانية / سيارات الشاشات", with permitted zones or hours.
- No 2025–2026 re-tender information found for the Riyadh taxi/bus ad concession.
- No information found for Jeddah, Dammam or Makkah taxi-ad concessions.

## 3. TGA rules for taxis, ride-hailing (توجيه) and limousines

### Takeaway
The TGA's consolidated "Regulation for Taxi, Taxi-Intermediary and Directing Activities" (اللائحة المنظمة لنشاط الأجرة ووسيط الأجرة والتوجيه) allows **in-vehicle** advertising, including an electronic screen. The conditions: it must not distract the driver or affect passenger safety or comfort, and it must comply with the municipal ad rules. Taxis must also carry a **TGA-specified external identity** (colour plus approved stickers). No TGA text expressly permitting or banning **exterior roof-top ad screens** was found. Unapproved stickers are fined under TGA schedules.

### Cited Findings
- **In-vehicle ads.** The text extracted: "مع مراعاة قواعد تنظيم لوحات الدعاية والإعلان الصادرة عن الجهة المعنية، يُسمح للناقل بالإعلان داخل المركبة على أن تكون المادة الإعلانية أو الشاشة الإلكترونية في مكان لا يسترعي انتباه السائق ولا يؤثر على سلامة الركاب وراحتهم". In English: subject to the advertising rules of the competent body, the carrier may advertise inside the vehicle, provided the material or electronic screen is placed where it does not draw the driver's attention or affect passenger safety and comfort. — [TGA regulation 1538](https://www.tga.gov.sa/Regulations/Read/1538); [Umm Al-Qura publication](https://www.uqn.gov.sa/details?p=23377)
- **Earlier version (older item).** Ads were allowed behind the front seat only, not above seat height, and had to comply with the advertising regulation issued by Royal Decree M/35. — per search extract of [qanoniah copy of earlier executive regulation](https://qanoniah.com/File/BJmxzW4K1LGAgJ4nvwQd5pNgk-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%86%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%B0%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%86%D8%B4%D8%B7%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D9%82%D9%84-%D8%A8%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%AC%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%B7%D8%A9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D9%86%D9%82%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D8%A8%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%AC%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D9%88%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%AD%D8%AA%D9%87-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%86%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%B0%D9%8A%D8%A9)
- **Ride-hailing.** The earlier ride-hailing regulation required the **driver's consent** to in-car ads, under the same placement limits. — [Istitlaa: ride-hailing regulation](https://istitlaa.ncc.gov.sa/ar/Transportation/tga/TGARIDE/Pages/default.aspx)
- **External identity.** Public taxis and airport taxis must have a specified external colour and explanatory stickers for the service provider, per a technical guide issued by the TGA President. — [Istitlaa: taxi executive regulation](https://istitlaa.ncc.gov.sa/ar/Transportation/tga/TGATAXII/Pages/default.aspx)
- **Amendment history.**
  - The regulation was amended by Minister of Transport Decision No. 1485 dated 20/11/1444H (mid-2023). — [issnad.sa summary, Nov 2023](https://issnad.sa/2023/11/08/%D8%A3%D8%A8%D8%B1%D8%B2-%D9%85%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%AD-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%85%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%86%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%AC/)
  - A further amendment was made by Decision No. 1798. — [decreesa](https://arabic.decreesa.com/p/493114/) (date not confirmed)
- **Fines for unapproved stickers and ad conditions.**
  - Search extracts cite "عدم الالتزام بشروط الإعلان داخل المركبة" (breaching the in-vehicle ad conditions) at **SAR 500**. The context and date were unclear.
  - A TGA schedule (trucks) lists fitting decorations or **TGA-unapproved stickers** inside or outside the vehicle at SAR 500. — [old.tga.gov.sa](https://old.tga.gov.sa/Regulations/Read?term=%D8%AA%D8%A3%D8%AC%D9%8A%D8%B1+%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA+%D9%88%D9%88%D8%B3%D8%B7%D8%A7%D8%A1+%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%A3%D8%AC%D9%8A%D8%B1)
  - A 2025 draft urban-bus regulation lists SAR 400 for unapproved stickers. — [ainalnaql, Aug 2025](https://ainalnaql.com/index.php/2025/08/20/76914/)
  - These figures are not confirmed for taxis.
- **Older TGA taxi specs (2018).** These required an *identification* screen inside the taxi showing operating-card data, plate and company; that is not an ad screen. — [TGA on X, 2018](https://twitter.com/Saudi_TGA/status/1065209653859155968?lang=ar) (older item)

### Inferences
- **In-car rear-seat screens** in taxis and ride-hailing cars look **permitted in principle**, subject to placement limits. For ride-hailing, driver consent likely applies (based on the earlier version). Municipal ad rules also apply.
- **Exterior taxi-top screens.** These must coexist with the mandatory TGA external identity, and any external sticker or device not approved by TGA is fineable. A roof screen would therefore realistically need TGA acceptance (likely via the taxi operator) plus municipal concession or licence plus Traffic approval.
- **Ride-hailing cars are privately registered vehicles.** Exterior screens on them would fall under the restrictive private-vehicle rule ("stickers on sides only").

### Gaps
- No TGA decision from 2024–2026 found that expressly addresses taxi-top digital ad screens (permitting or banning them).
- The current consolidated text and current TGA penalties schedule for taxis could not be read directly (tga.gov.sa was blocked in this session).
- The limousine-specific rule was not located separately; it is presumably covered by the same consolidated taxi regulation.

## 4. Moroor (General Department of Traffic): vehicle modifications, illuminated devices, stickers/wraps, approvals

### Takeaway
The Traffic Law treats any unauthorised alteration to a vehicle's body or structure, and any unauthorised device or light/sound warning device, as a violation. Ads or company logos on commercial vehicles have historically been allowed only within narrow limits and with permission. No Saudi traffic text was found that expressly authorises LED ad screens on vehicles. In practice, a screen needs Traffic approval, which the MOMRAH rules also require before the municipal licence.

### Cited Findings
- **Traffic Law provisions.**
  - Making any modification or addition to a vehicle's body or chassis that changes its features or basic equipment, without the statutory procedures, is a violation. The MOI schedule lists it with **impoundment until the violation is removed**.
  - Fitting or using unauthorised sound or **light** warning devices is prohibited. — [Traffic Law (BOE)](https://laws.boe.gov.sa/BoeLaws/Laws/LawDetails/85364e57-c01e-41ba-8def-a9a700f183e9/1?csrt=10181980062366402065); [MOI penalties page](https://www.moi.gov.sa/wps/portal/!ut/p/z0/fY7LDoIwFER_pZuu70UJCUsWPlATE12I3TQNFLgKLY-Cfr6Ae3ZzZiaTAQEJCKNGKpQja1Q18VMEEmPfP3r-5nzdRjuMgv0lvIXoxR7CXXVwArFemlbo1bYiApFa4_TXQVJbYhl9lOE4y9LWmi2hcRxn4tjpYqiWJxxdp_KcUjaS_Vs9k6zR00dHul825HpHZjpXQ-WkNtC8D48f0YqnPw!!/)
- **Unauthorised devices or stickers.** "Using unauthorised devices in the vehicle or placing logos or stickers contrary to public decency" is in violation schedule No. 4, per Saudi Traffic (@eMoroor) in 2019. — [eMoroor on X](https://x.com/eMoroor/status/1187098676667256832)
- **Fine amounts conflict across sources.**
  - The MOI page lists the 4th category at SAR 100–150 and the 1st category at SAR 500–900 or impoundment.
  - A law-firm blog places unauthorised body modification at SAR 1,000–2,000.
  - Fine tables were amended in recent years. Treat all figures as unverified. — [MOI](https://www.moi.gov.sa/wps/portal/!ut/p/z0/fY7LDoIwFER_pZuu70UJCUsWPlATE12I3TQNFLgKLY-Cfr6Ae3ZzZiaTAQEJCKNGKpQja1Q18VMEEmPfP3r-5nzdRjuMgv0lvIXoxR7CXXVwArFemlbo1bYiApFa4_TXQVJbYhl9lOE4y9LWmi2hcRxn4tjpYqiWJxxdp_KcUjaS_Vs9k6zR00dHul825HpHZjpXQ-WkNtC8D48f0YqnPw!!/); [sad-law.com](https://sad-law.com/%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AE%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF/)
- **Ad stickers on public vehicles (older item).** Traffic threatened a **SAR 3,000** fine for ad logos or stickers on public vehicles without official permission. The case cited was a Jeddah marketing firm paying car owners to put rear-window stickers on their cars. — [Al-Mowaten, Nov 2017](https://www.almowaten.net/2017/11/3-%D8%A2%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%81-%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%B9%D9%82%D9%88%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%B6%D8%B9-%D9%85%D9%84%D8%B5%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%85%D8%AE%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%81%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89/)
- **Company name on transport vehicles and buses (older item).** Traffic allowed writing a company name or trademark on transport vehicles and buses, limited to **30×30 cm**, without blocking vision or affecting safety. — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/article/864222)
- **Historic ban (about 13 years old, status uncertain).** An older report cites royal orders banning advertising on taxis and public vehicles, with Traffic continuing to ticket violating ad stickers. — [Chamber.sa news](https://www.chamber.sa/MediaCenter/News/Pages/%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%B9%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A4%D8%B3%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%AA.aspx). It has since been superseded in practice by the 2020 outdoor-ad strategy and the Riyadh taxi-ad concession (Section 2).
- **Non-official claim.** A Saudi car-accessory retailer says permitted vehicle stickers must be static, with no screens or lighting. This is not an official source. — [elitecar-sa.com](https://elitecar-sa.com/ar/blog/%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%AD-%D9%85%D9%84%D8%B5%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9/a-702638458)

### Inferences
- **LED screens likely need explicit approval.** An externally mounted LED screen is probably a "body modification or addition" and arguably an "unauthorised light device" unless Traffic has approved it. Without that approval it risks a fine plus impoundment.
- **The approval route.** The practical route is Traffic approval (MOI/Absher business services or the local Traffic department), then the Balady/Amanah licence, which the MOMRAH rules expressly require in that order.
- **Wraps and stickers** for commercial ads are viable but need permission. Expect the strictest scrutiny of illuminated or moving content, given driver-distraction concerns.

### Gaps
- No current MOI/Absher e-service was identified for approving ad screens on vehicles.
- No official Moroor statement (2024–2026) on LED or digital ad screens on vehicles was found.
- The current fine amounts need checking against the MOI's latest violations table.

## 5. Evidence of practice: existing Saudi operators

### Takeaway
The clearest documented Saudi practice is the **Riyadh taxi/bus advertising concession**, a sticker format held by Fadn Advertising Agency (Al Arabia) under 2-year contracts with Remat Al-Riyadh signed in 2024. No publicly documented, licensed operator of digital **taxi-top screens** or roaming **LED ad trucks** in Saudi Arabia was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Riyadh concession (Fadn/Al Arabia).** 500 taxis and buses, ad stickers, 2-year contracts, about SAR 6.84m and SAR 5.8m. — [Tadawul](https://www.saudiexchange.sa/wps/portal/saudiexchange/newsandreports/issuer-news/issuer-announcements/issuer-announcements-details/?locale=enar&anCat=91&anId=77778&cs=4071); [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/ar/article/articledetail/id/1697184)
- **Transit screens.** Saudi Signs Media (with Rotana Signs) holds the Riyadh Metro in-carriage digital screen contract: 2,688 screens across 183 trains. This is evidence of digital transit-media concessions, but it is not road vehicles. — [Campaign ME](https://campaignme.com/saudi-signs-media-and-rotana-signs-awarded-riyadh-metro-carriages-digital-screens/); [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/kt-network/saudi-signs-media-launches-digital-screen-network-inside-riyadh-metro-carriages)
- **Regional comparator (not Saudi).** Dubai RTA launched smart taxi-top digital billboards in 2021, starting with 100 National Taxi cars. — [Dubai Media Office](https://mediaoffice.ae/ar/news/2021/feb/23-02/rta-launches-smart-taxi-top-billboards-project)

### Inferences
- **No visible digital vehicle-ad market.** The Saudi model is concession-based, run by Amanah investment arms through Furas. Mobile digital vehicle advertising is a gap, which may be an opportunity, but it has no visible licensing precedent. Entry likely means bidding for, or partnering on, Amanah concessions rather than relying on a generic per-vehicle Balady licence.

### Gaps
- No Saudi LED ad-truck operator was found with a public licence trail.
- No ride-hailing in-car screen operator (for example, on Careem or Uber Saudi fleets) was found. Further searching on Arabic news sites that this session could not reach is recommended.
