**Changes since the earlier reports**
1. **Size rules for car-roof screens.** The amended Advertising Regulation text allows a **fixed, non-moving panel or electronic screen on a vehicle roof of at most 40 cm high × 120 cm wide**. The base may be at most 20 cm and does not count toward the area. Lighting must be self-contained and fixed, and General Traffic Department (GTD) approval is required. The earlier reports did not have these numbers.
2. **Size rules for in-vehicle screens.** In-vehicle ad screens are capped at **25 × 40 cm** and may not sit in front of the driver. This comes from 599/2023 as amended.
3. **A traffic offence exists for using a vehicle to advertise.** The MOI's own violations table lists **"using a motor vehicle for advertising without a permit"** (استعمال المركبة الآلية في الإعلان دون تصريح). The settlement is KD 20, or KD 45–75 in court. A separate item covers writings, stickers, images or **additions** on a vehicle without a permit, with a KD 50 settlement. Together these confirm that a traffic-side permit is a legal requirement in itself, not just a practice.
4. **Correction on Decision 109/2026.** It is a **Ministry of Commerce and Industry (MOCI)** decision on delivery *platforms*: issued 8 Jul 2026 and published in Kuwait Al-Youm issue 1799 on 12 Jul 2026. It is **not** an MOI traffic decision, and it repealed MOCI Decision 10/2026. A court annulled its Article 15 in a case brought by Talabat. The earlier reports gave it inconsistently as an MOI decision.
5. **Truck peak-hour ban for 2026–27.** From **1 Sep 2026 to 14 Jun 2027** the ban runs 6:30–9:00 and 12:30–15:30. From 15 Jun to 31 Aug 2027 it runs 12:30–15:30 only.
6. **New MOI ride-hailing rules.** **MOI Ministerial Resolution 893/2026** on passenger-transport apps was published 5 Jul 2026 and replaced 724/2020. It is relevant to car-top screens on app cars. No advertising clause was found in it.
7. **The December 2025 amendments are still not confirmed as gazetted.** No gazette number or date was found for the ministerial resolution that gives effect to them. The **base regulation in force is still Ministerial Resolution 599/2023 as amended by 223/2024**.

## Q1. Kuwait Municipality Advertising Regulation: vehicle articles, illuminated and electronic ads, fees, duration, procedure, documents

### Takeaway
All advertising on vehicles needs a Kuwait Municipality ad licence under the Advertising Regulation (Ministerial Resolution 599/2023, amended by 223/2024, with further amendments approved by the Municipal Council in late 2025).

The vehicle rules are type-specific:
- **Sides:** ads go on unused sides of the vehicle, without protruding.
- **Roof:** only a fixed, non-moving panel or e-screen of up to 40×120 cm.
- **Interior:** screens up to 25×40 cm, not in front of the driver.
- **Delivery motorcycles:** an identifying ad for the operator's own activity, which may be a fixed electronic panel with non-moving content.

GTD approval is a licensing condition for every vehicle type. The only published third-party "investment" vehicle-screen product is the taxi and public-transport licence (TR133): KD 30/m² for moving TV screens, KD 20/m² per face otherwise, and a KD 1,000 guarantee.

### Inferences
- **[INF] Taxi or car-top:** a roof unit is licensable only as a **non-moving** display of at most 40×120 cm. A video roof screen is not allowed under the reported amended text. TR133's "moving TV screen" category therefore most likely refers to **interior** screens (25×40 cm), not roof units. That reconciles the apparent contradiction in the earlier reports.
- **[INF] Delivery boxes:** the explicit motorcycle clause allows only static content and, on the "identifying ad of the activity only" wording, only the operator's own branding. A third-party rotating video box screen has no published category.
- **[INF] Trucks:** no truck category exists. The general vehicle rules (sides without protrusion, self-contained lighting, GTD approval) plus an electronic-screen fee would apply by analogy. A side-mounted screen is not barred outright by any text found, but the static-only pattern for roof and motorcycle screens suggests the Municipality and GTD would require static content while the truck is moving.
- **[INF] Documents to expect for any vehicle ad licence:**
  - Commercial licence of the applicant.
  - Valid vehicle registration (دفتر الملكية).
  - Contract between the advertiser or investor and the vehicle owner.
  - GTD approval letter.
  - Design drawing from an approved engineering office.
  - Ad image and dimensions.
  - Financial guarantee.

  This list is assembled from the clauses above; there is no single official list.

### Takeaway
The regulatory direction in 2025–26 is **tightening**:
- Higher fines.
- MOI brightness limits.
- A ban on electronic boards in residential areas and on bridges.
- A Cabinet order (April 2026) to overhaul public-space advertising and tender sites to international firms.

## Q3. Traffic Law (Decree-Law 67/1976, amended by Decree-Law 5/2025): modifications, lights, screens, stickers. Is an LED screen allowed, and what approvals are needed?

### Takeaway
The traffic side has **its own permit requirement**. The MOI violations table penalises "using a motor vehicle for advertising without a permit" and "writings, stickers, images or additions on vehicles without a permit". Driver-visible or driver-controlled screens have been treated as serious violations.

No published GTD technical standard for vehicle ad screens was found. Approval is case-by-case through the GTD, and Municipality licences explicitly require a GTD approval letter. The 2025 law raised fines generally and broadened impoundment powers.

- **[LAW]** Reporting on the 2025 law lists illegal lights and other banned devices, and changing colour or major parts, among prohibited acts. — [Arab Times](https://www.arabtimesonline.com/news/kuwait-revamps-traffic-law-new-penalties-for-violations-and-changes-to-legal-procedures)

- **[PRACTICE]** Vehicle licence renewal includes a technical inspection (KD 5). — [e.gov.kw RenewVehicleLicense](https://e.gov.kw/sites/kgoArabic/Pages/Services/MOI/RenewVehicleLicense.aspx) (extract from earlier session)

### Inferences
- **[INF] Is an LED screen allowed?** No Kuwaiti text bans it outright. A static display within the Municipality's size and position limits, with a GTD advertising permit and a Municipality licence, is lawful. A screen fitted without a GTD permit is at minimum a "vehicle used for advertising without a permit" and an "addition without a permit". It may also be treated as an illegal light or banned device, especially if it shows video or is visible or controllable by the driver.
- **[INF] Approvals sequence:**
  1. GTD, through the Technical Inspection Department (الفحص الفني): no-objection or permit for the addition and its use for advertising, with re-inspection if body or electrical changes are made.
  2. Municipality ad licence, which requires the GTD letter.
  3. Content clearances by sector (MOH, MOCI, and from 2027 the Ministry of Information).

### Gaps
- The exact article text of the executive regulations on added lights, colours and display devices was not retrieved.
- No GTD published criteria (brightness, size, motion) for approving ad screens were found.
- It is unverified whether the 2025 amendment changed the KD 20 / 45–75 advertising-permit offence.

## Q4. Taxis and app cars: who regulates them, and rules on taxi ads and roof signs

### Takeaway
Taxis are licensed and supervised by the **MOI GTD**, with **MOCI** commercial licensing of operators. App-based passenger transport is governed by **MOI Ministerial Resolution 893/2026**, published 5 Jul 2026.

Ads on taxis are **permitted with a Municipality licence plus GTD approval**:
- Sides: no protrusion.
- Roof: static panel of up to 40×120 cm.
- Interior screens: up to 25×40 cm, not in front of the driver.
- Investment ads: TR133 fees.

No ban on ads on taxis was found.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- **[INF] The best route in Kuwait is taxis.** An explicit legal path exists. The commercial counterparty is the **taxi company** (licence holder), which must itself hold a valid ad licence. The startup signs an investor–owner contract and pays TR133 fees.
- **[INF] Roof video is off the table** under the reported text. Plan roof units as static (or slowly changing static frames, if GTD accepts them) at 40×120 cm or smaller. Video goes on an interior 25×40 cm screen.
- **[INF] App cars under 893/2026** are often citizen-owned private vehicles, so each owner would need the side or roof licence. The compliance burden per car is high.

### Gaps
- It is unconfirmed whether GTD treats a roof screen that cycles static images as "non-moving".
- No list was found of taxi operators that currently hold TR133 licences.
- No text was found on whether app-car roof ads conflict with 893/2026 vehicle-appearance or identification requirements.

## Q5. Delivery motorbikes: 2024–2026 rules (company licensing, riders, boxes, roads, hours) and ads or screens on boxes

### Takeaway
Delivery motorbikes are run by **MOI-licensed delivery companies** under MR 271/2020 as amended. The companies own yellow-plated vehicles and sponsor their riders.

The GTD actively enforces several rules:
- **Mandatory rear lighting and reflective strips on boxes.**
- A reflective company vest and helmet.
- No ring roads or expressways.
- A summer midday ban.
- **Up to 60-day impoundment**, plus administrative action against companies.

The Municipality allows only an identifying, static (non-moving) electronic panel on delivery motorcycles (KD 40/yr) with GTD approval. MOCI Decision 109/2026 regulates platforms, not bikes.

  - Keep right; no lane-splitting.
  - No stopping on sidewalks or in no-parking zones.

  - Company reflective vest and helmet.
  - Up to **60-day impoundment**, and administrative action against companies with repeat offenders.
  - Sources: [Kuwait Times 51768](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/51768/kuwait/traffic-department-lists-rules-for-delivery-motorcycle-riders/); [MOI GDT](https://www.moi.gov.kw/GDT)

- **[LAW]** MOI Ministerial Resolution 1/2026 amended the rules on renting cars and motorcycles. — [Al-Jarida 136668](https://www.aljarida.com/article/136668). The resolution number is not certain from the extract, and it is relevant only if the startup rents bikes.

### Inferences
- **[INF]** A box screen must keep the **mandatory rear light and reflective strip** visible. A screen that replaces or obscures them is a direct, actively enforced violation.
- **[INF]** A third-party rotating video box fails both the Municipality text (identifying, non-moving content) and likely the GTD permit test. The compliant version is:
  - The fleet company holds the licence.
  - Static content.
  - GTD-approved hardware.
  - Fixed self-contained lighting.

  Whether third-party static ads are allowed under the KD 100 "via advertising companies" line remains a question for the Municipality.
- **[INF]** The impoundment risk (60 days) falls on the fleet company's bikes. Fleets will demand indemnities and GTD approval in hand before they agree to fitting.
- **[INF]** MOCI 109/2026 caps platform-to-merchant fees "including advertising". This does not cover third-party OOH on boxes, but platforms under margin pressure may seek box-ad revenue themselves, or block it.

- No text was found on whether the GTD will approve screens on boxes, and no instance of approval or refusal was found.

## Q6. Trucks: movement bans, time windows and heavy-vehicle rules relevant to ad trucks

GTD bans **trucks** from roads at peak hours. For **1 Sep 2026 – 14 Jun 2027** the ban runs **06:30–09:00 and 12:30–15:30**; for **15 Jun – 31 Aug 2027** it runs **12:30–15:30 only**. There is a separate Ramadan timetable, and Wafra Highway has its own hours.

## Q7. Penalties for unlicensed advertising (Municipality, traffic, content)

### Takeaway
Exposure stacks across regimes:
- **Municipality:** KD 3,000–5,000 for a commercial ad by any means without a licence, under the amended regulation. Also KD 500–3,000 for prohibited-goods or informational ads, KD 500 for event ads, and removal or confiscation at the violator's cost.
- **Traffic:** KD 20 settlement (KD 45–75 in court) for using a vehicle to advertise without a permit; KD 50 for unpermitted additions or stickers; up to 60-day impoundment for delivery bikes and two months for obstruction or illegal parking.
- **Media Law 102/2026:** reported KD 1,000–50,000 for unlicensed advertising content, from about April 2027.

### Cited Findings
- **[LAW per amended text]** KD 3,000–5,000 for a commercial ad without a licence; KD 500–3,000 for informational or business ads or those promoting prohibited goods; KD 500 for occasion ads; licence withdrawal after two warnings. — [Al-Anba 1329133](https://www.alanba.com.kw/1329133); [Times Kuwait](https://timeskuwait.com/municipality-finalizes-amendments-to-advertising-regulations-with-stricter-fines-and-new-licensing-rules/); [Times Kuwait](https://timeskuwait.com/municipal-council-approves-sweeping-amendments-to-advertising-regulations/)
- **[LAW, 2021 draft]** An earlier draft gave a KD 500–5,000 range. — [Al-Anba 27-08-2021](https://www.alanba.com.kw/ar/kuwait-news/municipal-council/1066530/)
- **[LAW]** Traffic: KD 20 / KD 45–75 for advertising use without a permit. — [MOI GDT violations](https://www.moi.gov.kw/gdt/trafficviolations)
- **[LAW, secondary]** Traffic: KD 50 for stickers, writings or additions without a permit. — [kuwaiteservices](https://kuwaiteservices.com/4100/%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%85%D8%AE%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B1/)
- **[PRACTICE]** Removal of unlicensed ads within 48 hours of a report (Jun 2026). — [Al-Rai](https://www.alraimedia.com/article/1768991/)
- **[LAW]** Media Law fines are reported at KD 1,000–50,000. — [Al-Dostour](https://al-dostour.com/kuwait-media-law-2026/); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/social-media/1853030/kuwaits-new-media-regulation-law-what-businesses-need-to-know)

### Inferences
- **[INF]** The traffic fine is trivial. The real traffic risk is **impoundment** (bikes up to 60 days, other vehicles up to 2 months) and the "serious violation" treatment of driver-visible screens. The real municipal risk is the KD 3,000–5,000 fine, possibly per ad or vehicle; how it counts is unclear.

### Gaps
- It is unknown whether the municipal fine is counted per vehicle, per screen or per campaign.
- The executive regulations of the Media Law, and whether outdoor-screen content falls within it, are not yet available.
