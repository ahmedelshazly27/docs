## 1. Kuwait: delivery companies, riders, residency and traffic rules (2024–2026)

### Takeaway
Kuwait's delivery sector is legally a licensed-company business: riders must hold residency on the same company they drive for, carry company stickers and uniform, and the MOI now punishes both riders (60-day impoundment) and companies. Article 19 of Residence Decree-Law 114/2024 makes working for anyone other than the sponsor a deportable violation, so an expatriate rider cannot lawfully take ad income directly from the startup; the payment must go to the licensed delivery company.

### Cited Findings
**Traffic / MOI rules on delivery vehicles and riders**
- MOI requirements (effective 1 Oct 2022, older but still the base rule): a sticker of the company to which the delivery vehicle belongs; the driver's residency must be on the same company he works for; health certificate; unified uniform; licence withdrawal on violation — [Al-Rai](https://www.alraimedia.com/ampArticle/1604983)
- Traffic official: riders need a motorcycle driving licence, helmet and a **reflective strip on the rear box**; otherwise the bike is treated as violating and administratively impounded — summarised in search extract from [Al-Rai](https://www.alraimedia.com/ampArticle/1604983) / [Al-Jarida](https://www.aljarida.com/articles/1642087437235552900/amp) (date of the official's statement unclear)
- Oct 2026 General Traffic Department rules/campaign: published rules call for **lighting on the rear delivery box with reflective strips behind it**; riders cited for having no box lights and no reflective vests — [Kuwait Times (rules)](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/51768/kuwait/traffic-department-lists-rules-for-delivery-motorcycle-riders/); [Kuwait Times (campaign)](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/52132/kuwait/traffic-campaign-targets-delivery-motorcycle-violations/)
- Oct 2026: MOI warned delivery companies and riders; violators face motorcycle impoundment up to 60 days and **administrative action against operating companies whose riders repeatedly offend** — [ARY News](https://arynews.tv/kuwait-alerts-delivery-bikers); [GDN](https://www.gdnonline.com/Details/1408667/Kuwait-cracks-down-on-delivery-drivers-violating-traffic-regulations); [Kuwait Times "Firms told to rein in riders"](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/51033/kuwait/other-news/firms-told-to-rein-in-riders/). Another report says "two months" — [Kuwait Times](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/50966/kuwait/delivery-drivers-face-2-month-vehicle-seizure-for-violations/amp)
- Summer ban: delivery motorcycles barred nationwide 11:00–16:00, 1 June–31 Aug (reported for 2025 and 2026) — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/world/gulf/midday-ban-delivery-bikes-june-1-kuwait); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/kuwait/kuwait-bans-delivery-bikes-during-peak-summer-heat-hours-1.500559094); [Arab Times](https://www.arabtimesonline.com/news/strict-ban-on-delivery-bikes-during-11-am-4-pm-in-kuwait-summers/)
- Traffic Decree-Law 5/2025 (in force April 2025): failing to use required lights fined KD 45–75; details deferred to executive regulations — [Arab Times](https://www.arabtimesonline.com/news/kuwait-revamps-traffic-law-new-penalties-for-violations-and-changes-to-legal-procedures). A secondary fines list gives KD 30 for "using lights other than those permitted by law" (conflicts with the above; unofficial site) — [kuwait.it.com fines list](https://kuwait.it.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Kuwait-Traffic-Fines-List-pdf.pdf)
- Fines are a heavy burden on riders ("massive fines wipe out delivery bikers' salaries") — [Arab Times](https://www.arabtimesonline.com/news/massive-fines-wipe-out-delivery-bikers-salaries-in-kuwait)
- No search found any Kuwaiti rule specifically on screens or aftermarket electronic devices on delivery motorcycles (see prior report for the Dec 2023 Al-Jarida item on unapproved screens on car windows being a "serious" violation).

**MOCI regulation of platforms / delivery companies**
- MOCI Ministerial Decision 109/2026, published in the Official Gazette 12 July 2026, regulates intermediary platforms for ordering and delivery; platform licence holders had to change activity to "Management of delivery services via electronic platforms" (ISIC-style code 532013) by 1 Sept 2026; caps commissions (17% / 10%) and delivery fees (KD 1) — [almaal.org](https://almaal.org/kuwait-delivery-platform-rules) (secondary summary). This resolves the earlier report's conflict: 109/2026 is an MOCI decision, per Arabic coverage.
- More than 250 delivery companies filed a grievance with the Commerce Minister asking to suspend the decision; they say it confuses "delivery platform" with "delivery company" and gives no protection to delivery companies that bear rider salaries, housing, vehicles and health insurance — [Al-Jarida 137453](https://www.aljarida.com/article/137453); [Al-Jarida 137485](https://www.aljarida.com/article/137485); [Al-Jarida 137368](https://www.aljarida.com/article/137368)
- Administrative Court annulled Article 15 (penalties) of Decision 109/2026 after a Talabat suit — [Kuwait Times](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/50539/kuwait/other-news/court-strikes-down-delivery-penalties-raising-fears-of-weak-enforcement/)
- Same Kuwait Times piece: platforms (Talabat, Keeta, Jahez) rely on 3PL providers that supply drivers and vehicles and are paid per order — [Kuwait Times](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/50539/kuwait/other-news/court-strikes-down-delivery-penalties-raising-fears-of-weak-enforcement/)
- MOCI also issued a decision regulating "free business" (freelance) activities — [Al-Jarida 150186](https://aljarida.com/article/150186) (content not verified)

**Residency / working for others**
- Under Decree-Law 114/2024 (Foreigners' Residence) and its executive regulations, working for an employer other than the sponsor without permission is a deportable violation under Article 19; Art. 38 of the executive regulations lists cases of deportation even with valid residency — [Arab Times](https://www.arabtimesonline.com/news/kuwaits-new-residency-regulations-specify-grounds-for-expat-deportation); [Arab Times](https://www.arabtimesonline.com/news/kuwait-lists-3-cases-expats-can-be-deported-despite-valid-residency); [Legit.ng](https://www.legit.ng/world/1739687-kuwait-lists-3-reasons-foreigners-deported-valid-residence-permit/)
- Penalties overview for the residency law — [Arab Times](https://www.arabtimesonline.com/news/kuwait-approves-new-law-on-foreign-residency-with-strict-penalties)
- 2026: Kuwait moving toward a "freelancer visa" (fees reported KD 750–1,000) to end visa trading; delivery services cited as a sector exposed to unfair competition — [Arab Times, Mar 2026](https://www.arabtimesonline.com/news/kuwait-moves-toward-freelance-visa-system-to-regulate-labor-market); [Lexis ME, Feb 2026](https://www.lexis.ae/2026/02/19/kwt-new-freelance-residency-scheme/); [Arab Times](https://www.arabtimesonline.com/news/kuwaits-freelance-visa-plan-seeks-to-end-visa-trading)
- Aug 2026: Kuwait reportedly suspended new freelance business licences in a crackdown on permit abuse, threatening deportation — [AGBI](https://www.agbi.com/law/2026/08/kuwait-threatens-deportation-in-clampdown-on-illegal-businesses/)
- An official described an investigation into a delivery-app company illegally employing 500+ workers and paying salaries through a network of expatriates — search extract from [Arab Times](https://www.arabtimesonline.com/news/kuwaits-freelance-visa-plan-seeks-to-end-visa-trading) cluster (exact article not confirmed)
- PAM part-time permit (second employer, up to 4 h/day, primary employer NOC) — per prior report: [Fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/kuwait-launch-of-part-time-work-permits.html)

### Inferences
- An expatriate rider receiving money from the startup for "carrying a screen" is very likely "working for another party" under Art. 19 — risk falls on the rider (deportation) and on the payer. Calling it "rent of box space" does not obviously help because the rider does not own the box/bike (the fleet does). Pay the licensed delivery company (B2B media lease), which may pass bonuses via payroll.
- Box-mounted lighting is mandatory, so a lit box per se is fine; the question is whether a video/LED screen counts as an unpermitted light/modification. Get written GTD approval.
- Fleet operators (250+ companies, squeezed by KD 1 fee cap and commission caps) are economically motivated to accept new per-box revenue — but face company-level MOI sanctions, so they will demand regulatory cover.
- The "freelance visa" (if enacted) could in future let some riders work independently, but it is not in force as a general route as of the sources found; treat as watch item.

### Gaps
- Could not read the full GTD Oct 2026 rule list (kuwaittimes blocked); no published box dimension/brightness/screen standard found.
- No text confirming a specific ban on riders working for multiple platforms; the operative constraint is the residency-on-same-company rule (2022) and Art. 19.
- No evidence on Kuwaiti nationals riding independently.
- No source on MOI rules for electrical devices/battery packs on motorcycles.

## 2. Saudi Arabia: TGA delivery-app regulation, rider requirements, vehicle/box specs and ad rules

### Takeaway
TGA requires non-Saudi couriers to work through licensed light-transport (delivery) companies in a TGA-approved unified uniform, restricts freelancing to Saudis, and tightly controls motorcycles (new motorcycle delivery licences frozen Oct 2024). The municipal Home Delivery permit (mandatory 1 July 2025) requires the establishment's name/brand clearly on the vehicle. The Saudi vehicle-ad rules (Cabinet Decision 590, ~2020) allow third-party ads on buses and taxis but only own-brand ads on commercial vehicles — which on its face excludes third-party ads on delivery bikes/cars.

### Cited Findings
**TGA delivery sector decisions**
- Jan 2024: TGA issued six regulatory decisions for the delivery sector — [Al Arabiya](https://www.alarabiya.net/aswaq/economy/2024/01/23/-النقل-السعودية-تصدر-6-قرارات-تنظيمية-لقطاع-توصيل-الطلبات); [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/business/transport-and-logistics/saudi-tga-issues-six-decisions-aimed-to-govern-online-delivery-sector-o7bvfsju) (older)
- Phase 1 effective 2 April 2024: non-Saudis must work through licensed light-transport companies (initially Al-Baha, Jazan, Najran, Northern Borders); freelance limited to Saudis; motorcycle controls with General Traffic Dept; unified driver uniform approved; phased rollout — [Saudi Gazette](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/641803); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-new-rules-for-delivery-services-sector-take-effect-1.101979587); [Al-Eqtisadiah](https://www.aleqt.com/2024/04/02/article_2714826.html); [Entarabi](https://entarabi.com/en/?p=22901) (older)
- Uniform: TGA approved two models — one for workers employed directly by licensed light-transport companies, one for the app-based delivery establishment; design requires TGA approval — search extract from [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/ampArticle/2168833) / [Ajel](https://ajel.sa/local/fz9y0um3xk) (older, 2024)
- Face-verification of drivers linked to TGA system required — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-new-rules-for-delivery-services-sector-take-effect-1.101979587)
- Motorcycle controls (TGA + General Traffic): valid operating card; **must not carry orders whose weight and dimensions affect road users**; no operating on prohibited roads; registration and technical specs — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/investigation/na/2168833); [Al-Muraba](https://www.almuraba.net/?p=604853)
- Oct 2024: TGA stopped issuing new motorcycle delivery licences for app orders — [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/1760983); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-freezes-new-motorcycle-delivery-licences-operating-through-mobile-apps-as-regulatory-changes-loom-1.1728986009688) (older)
- 2026 TGA circular: may block delivery apps used by restaurants/retailers that rely on manual dispatch or unlicensed vehicles; TGA warns apps against unlicensed carriers — search extract; [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/world/middle-east/saudi-tga-warns-of-penalizing-delivery-apps-that-ship-their-products-through-unlicensed-carriers-i8eo2zsq)
- HRDF freelance-delivery support for Saudis (own vehicle, TGA-approved, freelance certificate); launch listed 30 Sept 2025 — [HRDF](https://www.hrdf.org.sa/en/products-and-services/programs/individuals/enable/financial-aid-for-delivery-app-workers/apply/); [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/economy/gcc/financial-support-up-to-700-for-saudis-working-in-delivery-apps-u0mknzpg)
- Federation of Saudi Chambers warned restaurants/stores not to contract third-party delivery that doesn't meet TGA rules — search extract (source among [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/ampArticle/2153558) cluster; not individually verified)

**Box / vehicle specs**
- SASO draft standard **SASO DS 33715** covers requirements and test methods for food-keeping boxes used on delivery motorcycles (weight, drop tests etc.) — [SASO DS 33715](https://saso.gov.sa/ar/Laws-And-Regulations/public_multimedia/Documents/SASO-DS-33715.pdf) (draft status; dimensions not seen)
- Municipal "Home Delivery" permit via Balady mandatory from 1 July 2025: vehicle must be a car or bike equipped solely for home delivery; **establishment's name or trademark must be clearly displayed on the vehicle**; vehicles must meet technical and health requirements; courier must carry the establishment's permit — [Sabq](https://sabq.org/article/TdI2UJ6); [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/news/local/2202338); MoMAH document [الاشتراطات البلدية لخدمة التوصيل المنزلي (Feb 2025)](https://momah.gov.sa/sites/default/files/2025-02/alashtratat%20albldyt%20lkhdmt%20altwsyl%20almnzly.pdf) (not opened; blocked)

**Advertising on vehicles**
- Cabinet Decision 590 (12/9/1441H ≈ May 2020, older) approved the Outdoor Advertising Development Strategy; cancelled the 1430H ban on ads on taxis and other vehicles; amended the 1412H Rules for Advertising Signs to allow ads on buses and public taxis within cities **for parties other than the vehicle owner**, and added a paragraph allowing ads on **commercial vehicles within cities for the vehicle owner itself** — [Ajel](https://ajel.sa/local/nfvjxc); [Ajel](https://ajel.sa/local/zqv3s9); [Al-Muraba](https://www.almuraba.net/?p=328828)
- Rules text: [قواعد تنظيم لوحات الدعاية والإعلان (Qanoniah)](https://qanoniah.com/File/mJy4DGw9da7v68J78Qv65RrKX-قواعد-تنظيم-لوحات-الدعاية-والإعلان)
- MoMAH advertising-sign requirements exclude signs installed inside moving vehicles — [Madinah Amanah PDF](https://services.amana-md.gov.sa/DigitLibPanel/StaticFile/PdfFile//9eec7309-c603-4785-97cf-2b29fe992fa6.pdf); [MoMAH 2022](https://momah.gov.sa/sites/default/files/2022-03/%D8%A7%D8%B4%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B7%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%84%D9%88%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%A9.pdf)
- Traffic departments have historically fined company vehicles bearing promotional stickers (pre-2020 report) — [Ajel](https://ajel.sa/local/nfvjxc)
- Riyadh Amanah adopted an advertising plan including ads on taxis and buses under the strategy — search extract (Ajel cluster)

### Inferences
- Read literally, third-party ads on a delivery bike/car (a "commercial vehicle") are not permitted; only the owner's own brand. Routes to explore: (a) a licensed outdoor-advertising investor via a Balady/Furas contract treating delivery boxes as a new medium; (b) ads for the vehicle owner's own brand (e.g., the platform or fleet) — which is what platforms could sell to themselves/partners; (c) seek a TGA + MoMAH + Traffic joint approval. Low confidence: whether delivery motorcycles registered to light-transport companies fall under "commercial vehicle".
- Paying non-Saudi riders directly is barred both by labour/residency (iqama-sponsor) rules and by TGA's company-only requirement; Saudi freelancers (own vehicle, TGA-approved) are the only individual riders who could contract directly — but their boxes must still carry required branding and comply with ad rules.
- TGA's uniform approval and the Balady brand-display rule mean the box surface is already partly regulated real estate.

### Gaps
- Whether TGA phase 2/3 (all regions, company-only for non-Saudis) has been fully implemented by 2026 — not found.
- Exact TGA box dimensions and electrical device rules — not found; SASO DS 33715 status unknown.
- No specific TGA rule on ads on delivery boxes found; no Balady rule specifically on delivery-box ads found.
- Full text of لائحة تنظيم نشاط توصيل الطلبات عبر التطبيقات not retrieved.

## 3. Platform policies and platforms' own box/bag ad products

### Takeaway
No public rider/fleet-partner term was found that expressly bans third-party ads on boxes, but Talabat (and peers) actively police rider uniform, gear and "brand compliance" through 3PLs, and Talabat already monetises box screens itself (Flyby, Dubai). A rider-level ad business would conflict with platform kit rules; platforms are more likely competitors or channel partners.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- Platforms will treat the box surface as brand/media inventory; precedent (Flyby, foodpanda) shows the box-screen model is sold to or through platforms. A rider- or fleet-level product placed on platform-branded boxes without consent risks deactivation of riders and contractual claims against fleets.
- Competition angle: if a dominant platform (Talabat in Kuwait, HungerStation/Keeta/Jahez in KSA) contractually forbids fleets from third-party box ads while selling its own, this could be examined under Kuwait's Competition Law and Saudi GAC rules — speculative; no case found.
- Multi-app riders/fleets (Keeta and Talabat share 3PLs) suggest neutral/unbranded boxes are common among 3PLs that serve several apps, which may be the opening.

### Gaps

## 4. Fleet / 3PL companies as counterparties

### Takeaway
In both countries the riders sit inside licensed fleet companies (Kuwait: 250+ delivery companies; KSA: TGA-licensed light-transport companies and platform logistics arms), making B2B contracts with fleets the legally viable route.

### Cited Findings
- Kuwait: more than 250 delivery companies organised a joint grievance against MOCI 109/2026 — [Al-Jarida](https://www.aljarida.com/article/137453)
- Kuwait: Keeta (launched 15 Sept 2025) used local logistics partners; partner companies recruited riders (one advertising KD 250 base salary) — [Momentum](https://thelowdown.momentum.asia/meituans-keeta-food-delivery-lands-in-kuwait-third-stop-in-the-middle-east/); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/kuwait/chinese-food-delivery-giant-keeta-launches-in-kuwait-with-50-per-cent-discounts-1.500270898)
- Saudi: Keeta 38,000+ riders across 23 cities (2025); 3PL "DLC" (Dammam) moved 61 of 70 riders onto Keeta by Oct 2025 — [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/keeta-boosts-dlc-rider-fleet-from-20-to-61-1510885); [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/1886411)
- Saudi: Jahez's logistics arm Logi targets fulfilling 60% of Jahez orders by 2026 — search extract ([Redseer](https://redseer.com/articles/ksa-food-aggregators-leap-ahead-in-growth-journey/) cluster; not individually verified)
- Saudi precedent for licensed moving-vehicle screens via a transport company: MediaGo + Jeddah Transport Co. (per prior report) — [Campaign ME](https://campaignme.com/mediago-mobile-dooh-platform-jeddah/)

### Inferences
- Kuwait: contract with delivery-company licence holders (owners of bikes and sponsors of riders); 3PLs serving multiple apps may have unbranded boxes. Saudi: contract with TGA-licensed light-transport companies, and with Saudi freelancers only individually; platform logistics arms (Logi, HungerStation's fleet) are platform-controlled and less likely to host competitors' screens.

### Gaps
- No public list of Kuwaiti or Saudi 3PLs with fleet sizes; no example of a 3PL already selling box ads in either country.

## 5. Safety: electrical devices, batteries, weight, lighting

### Takeaway
Rules exist on load weight/dimensions (KSA) and mandatory box lighting/reflectors (Kuwait), plus battery product standards, but no specific rule on LED screens on motorcycles was found in either country.

### Cited Findings
- KSA: no orders with weight/dimensions affecting road users — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/investigation/na/2168833)
- KSA: SASO DS 33715 delivery-box standard (draft) includes weight/drop tests — [SASO](https://saso.gov.sa/ar/Laws-And-Regulations/public_multimedia/Documents/SASO-DS-33715.pdf)
- KSA batteries technical regulation (SASO IEC 62133-2) — [SASO TR](https://saso.gov.sa/en/Laws-And-Regulations/Technical_regulations/Documents/TR-Electric-Batteries.pdf)
- Kuwait: rear box lighting + reflective strips required; KD 45–75 fine for not using required lights; unofficial KD 30 for non-permitted lights — [Kuwait Times](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/51768/kuwait/traffic-department-lists-rules-for-delivery-motorcycle-riders/); [Arab Times](https://www.arabtimesonline.com/news/kuwait-revamps-traffic-law-new-penalties-for-violations-and-changes-to-legal-procedures); [kuwait.it.com](https://kuwait.it.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Kuwait-Traffic-Fines-List-pdf.pdf)

### Inferences
- Design to: static images, auto-dimming, no red/blue flashing colours, retain the required rear lamp and reflectors, low weight, certified Li-ion pack, heat management for 45°C+ summers.

### Gaps
- No Kuwait or KSA rule found on brightness, colours or animation for screens on motorcycles; no rule found on maximum box weight in Kuwait.
