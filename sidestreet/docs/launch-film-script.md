# side street — launch film script (v3)

**Length:** ~45s · **Format:** 16:9 (cut-down 9:16 for Reels) · **Language:** Kuwaiti Arabic, English subtitles
**Setting:** Night, quiet side street in Salmiya. Three friends at the open boot of a white Land Cruiser with karak.

**Cast**
- **Yousef** — always hungry, never wrong. White dishdasha, round face, short beard.
- **Hamad** — always about to buy something. Grey dishdasha, slim, stubble.
- **Bu Fahad** — thinks he knows everything. Navy dishdasha, glasses, fuller beard.

Screen brands are fictional placeholders: Dar Al Mashawi (car top), Marfa Bank (truck), Fanar Chalets (bike box).

## Fight 1 — food
| Shot | Line |
|---|---|
| Wide, three at the car | **Yousef:** خلاص، نطلب مشاوي. — *That's it, we're ordering grills.* |
| CU Hamad | **Hamad:** مشاوي مرة ثانية؟! أمس مشاوي، أول أمس مشاوي! — *Grills AGAIN? Yesterday grills, the day before grills!* |
| CU Yousef, offended | **Yousef:** وشنو المشكلة؟ أنا ولد مشاوي. — *So? I'm a grills kind of guy.* |
| Bu Fahad on his phone | **Bu Fahad:** اطلبوا سوشي وخلصونا. — *Just order sushi and spare us.* |
| Yousef + Hamad | **Both:** سوشي؟! — *SUSHI?!* |
| Cutaway: car-top screen glides past — Dar Al Mashawi, "family platters for people who can't agree" | (they freeze) |
| Yousef, smug | **Yousef:** شفتوا؟ حتى الشاشة معاي. — *See? Even the screen's on my side.* |

## Fight 2 — new or used car
| Shot | Line |
|---|---|
| CU Hamad | **Hamad:** أبي أبدل سيارتي. — *I want to change my car.* |
| Bu Fahad | **Bu Fahad:** اشتر مستعملة. الجديدة تخسر نص قيمتها أول ما تطلع من الوكالة. — *Buy used. A new one loses half its value the second it leaves the dealer.* |
| Hamad | **Hamad:** مستعملة؟ عشان أعيش في الكراج؟ — *Used? So I can live at the garage?* |
| Yousef, mouth full | **Yousef:** أنت ما تقدر تختار عشا، تبي تختار سيارة؟ — *You can't even pick dinner and you want to pick a car?* |
| Cutaway: LED truck rolls past — Marfa Bank, "New or used? Marfa finances both" | (heads turn slowly) |
| Hamad, quietly | **Hamad:** …خلاص، الموضوع انتهى. — *…Okay, case closed.* |

## Fight 3 — the weekend
| Shot | Line |
|---|---|
| Bu Fahad, excited | **Bu Fahad:** الويكند نسوي كشتة. — *This weekend, we go camping in the desert.* |
| CU Yousef | **Yousef:** كشتة؟ آخر مرة غرزنا بالرمل ست ساعات! — *Camping? Last time we were stuck in the sand for six hours!* |
| Bu Fahad | **Bu Fahad:** هذيك غلطة حمد، هو اللي كان يسوق. — *That was Hamad's fault, he was driving.* |
| Hamad, pointing | **Hamad:** أنت اللي قلت "قص من هني، أعرف الطريق"! — *YOU said "cut through here, I know the way"!* |
| Cutaway: delivery bike stops at kerb — Fanar Chalets, "Khairan. Book tonight" | (silence) |
| Yousef, quietly | **Yousef:** …شاليه. — *…Chalet.* |
| Bu Fahad + Hamad | **Both:** شاليه. — *Chalet.* |

## Ending
| Shot | Line |
|---|---|
| Wide, short silence | **Hamad:** انزين، منو بيطلب العشا؟ — *So… who's ordering dinner?* |
| Arguing again over each other | **Yousef:** مشاوي! **Hamad:** لا! — *Grills! / No!* |
| Camera pulls back; a car passes with only the side street frame logo on its roof screen | (argument fades under street sound) |
| End card, black | **side street** · moving screens across Kuwait · Join the waiting list |
