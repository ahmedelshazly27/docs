# side street launch film: direction and edit rules

Based on a shot-by-shot breakdown of the SeenX "Be seen. Be everywhere." launch film (16 shots in 42s, median shot 2.2s, hard cuts only) and the raw, cut-driven style of brands like Corteiz.

## Edit
- **Hard cuts only.** No crossfades, dissolves, fades between shots, slow motion or zoom transitions.
- **Shot length:** dialogue 1.2–3s; screen cutaways 2–3s; opening wide ~3s; the final wide holds 5–6s.
- **Comedy timing:** cut on the line, then hold the listener's reaction for 0.5–1s of silence before the next line. The joke lands on the face, not on the words.
- **One punch-in maximum,** and only on a punchline (e.g. "سوشي؟!"). It is a sharp cut to a tighter frame, never an animated zoom.
- **Title:** hard cut to black, logo on screen, no animation flourish. Fade the audio out under the last 1s only.

## Camera
- **Dialogue:** over-the-shoulder close-ups and singles, the foreground shoulder soft and out of focus. Mostly locked off; subtle handheld at most.
- **Screen cutaways:** low, at car height, a slow drift alongside the vehicle; only the screen is lit; the screen light spills on the asphalt.
- **Ending:** locked wide of the majlis; a car with only the side street frame logo on its roof screen passes in the background.

## Look and sound
- Night, warm sodium street light with cold white LED from the carports; lantern glow on faces.
- Phone/indie-film realism: natural skin, no beauty lighting, no heavy grade.
- Natural sound only: street ambience, cups, distant cars, engine hum on cutaways. No music under dialogue; an optional short sting on the end card.

## Generation workflow
1. Approved master frame → close-up and over-the-shoulder stills of each character (same scene, same light).
2. One 3–4s clip per line or reaction, starting from the matching still (Gemini Omni Flash 1.2, image-to-video, locked camera, line spoken in Kuwaiti Arabic).
3. Screen cutaways generated separately; the ad creative is placed on the screen.
4. Edit with hard cuts, subtitle in English, end card.
