## About / Overview
**English**

side street is Kuwait's moving screen network. We put bright LED screens on trucks, delivery-bike boxes and car tops, and take your brand into the neighbourhoods where people actually live, eat and park — Salmiya, Hawalli, Jabriya, Sharq, Jahra and beyond.

Billboards own the highway. We go where they can't: café streets, co-op car parks, mall circuits and the blocks behind the main road.

How it works:
• Pick your screens — trucks for impact, bike boxes for neighbourhood reach, car tops for frequency.
• Pick the areas, roads and hours your customers are out.
• Upload your creative — we check it against Kuwait's advertising rules and the screen specs.
• Get the play report — every play logged with time and street.

We're launching in Kuwait with a limited fleet. Brands on the waiting list get first pick of areas and launch pricing. Own a fleet, a delivery company or a shopfront? Join as a screen partner.

Join the waiting list: [website link]
